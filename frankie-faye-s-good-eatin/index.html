The list

What we make.

Gulf shrimp po'boy

Fried Gulf shrimp on a toasted French roll, dressed with shredded lettuce, tomato, pickles, and house remoulade.

Fried catfish plate

Two fillets, hush puppies, creamy coleslaw, and your choice of hot sauce or tartar on the side.

Smothered chicken + rice

Bone-in thigh in brown onion gravy over steamed white rice, served with a square of cornbread.

Loaded mac + cheese bowl

Three-cheese sauce over elbow pasta, topped with pulled pork or crispy fried okra depending on the day.

Banana pudding cup

Layered with vanilla wafers, fresh banana slices, and scratch whipped cream. Made fresh every morning before the truck rolls.

A short note

From Ashley.

Ashley has been cooking Gulf Coast food since before she had a truck to serve it from. Frankie Faye's started as a name on a catering tray and turned into a route she runs most days of the week from Biloxi to Gulfport. She makes the banana pudding fresh every morning and picks up shrimp on the way.

— Ashley

Book Frankie Faye's Good Eatin.

We started Frankie Faye's because most places around Biloxi that do Gulf Coast comfort food are either a sit-down spot with a thirty-minute wait or a drive-through with no soul. We run the truck so people can get a real plate on their lunch break without making a reservation. The route changes to fit the week, but the food stays the same.
