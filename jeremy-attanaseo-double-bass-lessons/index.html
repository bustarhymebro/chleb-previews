About

How Northbrook families find their bass teacher

We started teaching out of Northbrook because most families looking for a double bass teacher end up with whoever is available, not someone who actually plays and teaches the instrument seriously. A few hundred families in the area have gone through lessons here, from beginning fifth graders to adults finally learning the instrument they always wanted to play. Every lesson plan gets built around the student in front of us, not a one size fits all method book.

Meet Jeremy

Jeremy played double bass through Northwestern and four years in a regional orchestra pit before he started teaching out of his Northbrook studio. He won't move a student past open strings until the bow hand is right, even if the parents want faster recital pieces.
