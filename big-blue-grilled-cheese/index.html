How we run the truck in Sarasota

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Check the weekly schedule on the truck or call. We post location the night before.
Order at the window
Tell us the sandwich, add bisque if you want it. Cash or card both fine.
Pick up hot
Eight to ten minutes on the griddle. We hand it to you wrapped, cut, ready to eat.

The list

What we make.

The Big Blue

Sharp cheddar, blue cheese crumble, caramelized onion, fig jam on sourdough. Grilled in butter, cut on the diagonal.

Three cheese classic

Cheddar, muenster, american on thick-cut sourdough. The one we sell the most of, no apologies.

Short rib melt

Slow-braised short rib, gruyere, horseradish aioli, pickled red onion on rye. Heavy. Worth it.

Tomato bisque cup

House tomato basil bisque, eight ounce cup with a side wedge for dipping. Vegetarian, gluten-free bread on request.

Kids grilled cheese combo

Plain american on white, apple slices, juice box. Half size, half price, the only thing on the menu under five dollars.

A short note

From Brenna.

Brenna started Big Blue out of a single griddle and a borrowed canopy at the Sarasota farmers market. She runs the truck herself most shifts, answers her own phone for bookings, and refuses to use pre-shredded cheese.

— Brenna

Book Big Blue Grilled Cheese.

We started Big Blue because Sarasota had ten taco trucks and zero good grilled cheese. Every event organizer in town was asking the same question and nobody was answering it. We park where the people are, we cook every sandwich to order, and we keep the menu small enough that we actually do it well.
