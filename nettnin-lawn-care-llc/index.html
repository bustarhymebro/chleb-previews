The plan list

How we run yards in Brooksville

  • Weekly mow + edge package
    Mow, string trim fence lines, edge driveway and walks, blow clean. Flat weekly rate, billed monthly.
  • Bi-weekly maintenance plan
    Same scope as weekly, on a 14 day cycle. Best for shaded yards and snowbird properties off-season.
  • One-time reset cut
    For lawns that got away. Knock it back, bag the clippings, get it back on a normal mow height the next visit.
  • Mulch refresh by the yard
    Bed edges recut, fresh mulch laid two inches deep. Pine bark, cypress, or red, your call.
  • Palm and oak trim
    Lower fronds and crossing branches off, hauled to the curb. Anything over twenty feet we sub to a licensed climber.
