The list

What we make.

Chef-inspired hot sandwiches

Pressed and griddled to order. Rotating board built around what is good that week, served hot off the flat top.

Bao buns

Steamed pillowy buns folded around braised and crispy fillings. Two to an order, sauce on the bun, not on the side.

Parisienne sandwich

Brie, fruit preserves, bacon, and spinach pressed warm. The sweet-and-savory one regulars come back for.

Pasta salad

Cold side, dressed the morning of service. Pairs with any sandwich on the board, sold by the cup or pint.

Loaded potato salad

Yukon golds, bacon, cheddar, scallion, and the dressing pulled together in-house. Heavier side for the bigger appetite.

Sweet treats

House dessert of the day. Small batch, made the night before, gone when the case is empty.

A short note

From Paul.

Paul is the chef behind the window. He started Bistro Eats Dothan to bring real chef-driven sandwiches and bao to a town where lunch usually means a drive-thru. He builds the board himself, preps every morning, and won't send a plate out he wouldn't eat standing up.

— Paul

Book Bistro Eats Dothan.

We started Bistro Eats Dothan because lunch in this town leaned heavy on drive-thrus and chains, and the chef-driven side was missing. We park where there's foot traffic, keep the board short on purpose, and cook every ticket to order. If the bao is gone by one o'clock, that's the day we had.
