How we run yards in Dodge City

  • Weekly mow + edge
    Every 7 days April through October, mowed, string-trimmed, and blown off the driveway and porch.
  • Bi-weekly mow package
    Every other week for lawns that don't need the full weekly treatment, still edged and cleaned up each visit.
  • Spring cleanup
    Dead growth cut back, beds cleared, first mow of the season done before the grass gets away from you.
  • Fall leaf removal
    Leaves bagged or blown and hauled off the property, usually two to three passes once the trees let go.
