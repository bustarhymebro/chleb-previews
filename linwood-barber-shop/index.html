The list

How the chair has run for thirty years

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Men's haircuts
Standard cut, wash optional, neck shave, talc finish. The most common ask at the shop and the bread and butter for thirty years.
Fades
Skin, low, mid, or high. Clean taper at the sideburns and nape, blended with the top so the line never sits flat.
Beard work
Trim, shape, and line-up. Hot towel and beard oil if you want the full treatment. Walk-in friendly between cut appointments.
Cut and beard combo
Haircut plus beard work in the same seat. Saves a trip and lines both up against each other so the shape reads right.
Boys' cuts
Kids ten and under. Same chair, shorter sit. Duane has cut three generations on Linwood Road and knows how to keep a kid still.

The ritual

A seat in the chair.

01
Call or walk in

Phone rings to the shop. Walk-ins welcome between booked seats, especially mid-morning and after the lunch break.

02
Sit and talk

Quick read of what you want, what's grown out, what to leave alone. No upsell, no fuss.

03
Cut, line, finish

Clippers, scissors, straight razor on the neck, talc and a brush-off. Out the door looking like yourself, sharper.
