The menu

How we run the chair in Riverview

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Haircut + fade
Consult, cut, fade or taper, neck and sideburn line, finish styled. Roughly twenty-three minutes in the chair.
Beard shave
Hot towel prep, straight razor shave or shape, cold towel and balm to close. Beard line cleaned in.
Line-up
Crisp edge around the hairline and beard between full cuts. Quick stop to keep the shape sharp.
Hot towel wash + scalp massage
Warm wash at the basin, scalp massage, towel dry. Pairs with any cut or stands alone after a long week.
Hair coloring
Grey blending or full color matched in the chair, processed, washed out, and cut in the same visit.
