The menu

How Riverview gets cut here

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Skin fade + beard line-up
Bald fade through the sides, scissor work on top, beard edged to a clean line. Around 45 minutes in the chair.
Classic taper + part
Tapered sides left a touch longer, hard part if you want one, combed and finished with pomade.
Kids cut (under 12)
Patient cut for first-timers and regulars. Clipper or scissor, neckline cleaned, lollipop on the way out.
Buzz + hot shave
One-guard buzz across the head paired with a full straight-razor shave. The full reset, about 40 minutes.
Senior trim
Easy chair time for the regulars. Cut, neck shave, ear and brow tidy, no rush through it.
