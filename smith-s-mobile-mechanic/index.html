About

How we run jobs in Spring Hill

We started Smith's because every shop on US-19 runs on a wait list and a voicemail, and a working person cannot drop a vehicle for five days to get a starter swapped. We come to your driveway, your office lot, or wherever the car died, and we finish the job that day. A few hundred regulars across Hernando County keep us busy, and the phone still gets answered by the guy doing the work.

Meet Owner

Smith has been turning wrenches for over a decade across Hernando and Pasco County, and started running mobile after watching local shops book three weeks out for a brake job. He picks up his own phone, rolls up with the parts already in the van, and will not take a job he cannot finish in the driveway that day.
