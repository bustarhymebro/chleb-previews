Pinky's Tacos 806 runs a small mesquite-fired taco truck around Lubbock, serving handmade tortillas, slow-braised meats, and salsas built fresh that morning.

How Pinky's runs the route in Lubbock

What happens when you call.

Check the stops
Weekly schedule goes up Sunday night. Pick a lot, a brewery, or a lunch park near you.
Order at the window
Tell us meat, salsa, and how many. Most plates hit the counter in five minutes.
Eat and roll
Pull up a tailgate or a picnic table. Tips go in the jar, leftovers rarely happen.

The list

What we make.

Mesquite brisket taco

Smoked overnight, hand-chopped, double corn tortilla, white onion, cilantro, lime, choice of red or green salsa.

Al pastor

Marinated pork off the trompo with charred pineapple, onion, cilantro, salsa verde. Three to a plate with rice and beans.

Barbacoa plate

Slow-braised beef cheek, fresh corn tortillas, lime, pickled red onion, side of consomé for dipping.

Pollo asado

Citrus-marinated chicken thigh, grilled to order, charred onion, avocado salsa, two flour or corn tortillas.

Loaded street fries

Hand-cut fries, choice of meat, queso, pico, crema, jalapeño, cilantro. Built for sharing or not.
