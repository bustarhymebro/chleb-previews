How we run the truck in Riverview

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Check our location for the day, we post the corner and the hours by the night before.
Order at the window
Cash or card, we call your name when the plate is up. Salsas are self-serve.
Eat hot
Grab a seat at the picnic tables or take it to go in foil and a bag.

The list

What we make.

Tacos al pastor

Pork shaved off the trompo, double corn tortilla, pineapple, onion, cilantro, and the green salsa we make in the morning.

Carne asada plate

Skirt steak grilled over flame, rice, charro beans, two handmade tortillas, grilled jalapeño and onion on the side.

Quesabirria trio

Three corn tortillas dipped in birria fat, melted Oaxaca cheese, shredded beef, with a small cup of consomé for dipping.

Torta cubana

Telera bread, milanesa, ham, chorizo, queso, avocado, beans, jalapeños. The one we tell first-time customers to split.

Elote loco

Grilled corn off the cob, mayo, cotija, lime, tajín, hot sauce. Served in a cup with a spoon, no shirt mess.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner has been cooking this food for the family long before the truck had a name on it. The recipes come from home, the trompo gets set up before sunrise on weekends, and the salsas are made fresh every morning of service.
