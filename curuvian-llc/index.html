The list

What we make.

Smashburger combo

Two seared smash patties, American cheese, house sauce, pickles, brioche bun. Served with crinkle fries and a drink.

Cuban-style pork bowl

Slow-roasted mojo pork over rice and black beans, plantains, pickled onion, cilantro lime crema on the side.

Blackened grouper tacos

Three corn tortillas, blackened Gulf grouper, slaw, chipotle aioli, lime. Comes with chips and pickled jalapeños.

Birria quesadilla

Slow-braised beef and Oaxaca cheese pressed on the flat top, dipped in consomé. Includes onion, cilantro, and lime.

Event package, 50 guests

Two proteins, two sides, rolls, drinks, disposable plateware, two staff on site for two hours of service.

A short note

From Michelle.

Michelle has been cooking on the line in Sarasota kitchens for over a decade before putting Curuvian on the road. She runs the truck herself most days, books her own events, and won't take a date she can't personally cook.

— Michelle
