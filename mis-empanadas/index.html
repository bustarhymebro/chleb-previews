Mis Empanadas

Mis Empanadas is a Port Richey food truck running event service and catering across Pasco County, built around a short menu cooked the way Herbert's family makes it.

How we run events in Port Richey

What happens when you call.

You call
Tell us the date, the headcount, and the kind of event. Herbert picks up or calls back same day.
We build the menu
You get a written quote with the dishes, the per-head price, and the arrival time, usually within twenty-four hours.
We pull up and cook
Truck arrives early, sets up, and serves hot off the line until your guests are fed.

The list

What we make.

Authentic Puerto Rican food

Recipes Herbert grew up on. Sofrito made in-house, pernil slow-roasted overnight, rice cooked in the pot the right way.

Event-based food truck service

Festivals, block parties, church events, car meets. Generator on board, two-hour minimum, we handle the setup and breakdown.

Catering for events

Drop-off or full-service. We build a menu around your headcount, dietary notes, and timing, then bring everything hot.

Empanada platters

Trays of forty or eighty, mixed fillings, cut and labeled. Good for office lunches and showers when the truck does not fit.

Custom event menus

Tell us the occasion and the guest count, we send a menu sheet with options at a few price points within a day.

A short note

From Herbert.

Herbert started Mis Empanadas in 2021 to cook the food he grew up on for his neighbors in Port Richey. Five years in, the truck is event-based only, no storefront, no walk-up window. He answers the phone himself and books the calendar around the food he can actually do right.

— Herbert
