The list

What we make.

Beef picadillo empanada

Ground beef, sofrito, green olive, and raisin, folded by hand and fried to order. Sold by the piece or the dozen.

Chicken guisado empanada

Slow-stewed chicken with peppers, onion, and adobo, tucked inside our house dough. Comes with chimichurri on the side.

Ham and cheese empanada

Diced ham and melted queso in a golden shell. The kid favorite. Stays crisp through a thirty minute drive home.

Guava and cheese empanada

Sweet guava paste and cream cheese, dusted with sugar. Made through the afternoon, sells out most Saturdays before three.

Catering tray of fifty

Mixed savory empanadas, two house sauces, plates and napkins. Drop-off hot or set up at your spot for an hour.

A short note

From Owner.

Mis Empanadas is a family operation working the Port Richey and New Port Richey lots most days of the week. The owner picks up the phone between orders, folds every empanada by hand that morning, and won't sell anything that came out of the freezer.

— Owner

Book Mis Empanadas.

We started Mis Empanadas because every Latin spot on this side of Pasco closes early or runs a long line out the door, and folks around Port Richey deserve a hot empanada without the wait. The truck moves between a few regular lots, the dough is made every morning, and the recipes come from family. Call ahead and your order is bagged when you walk up.
