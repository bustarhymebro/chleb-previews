A short note

From Owner.

Jolly Frog started as a one-truck idea built around a simple premise: Madison's lunch hour is forty-five minutes, not two hours, and the city deserved better than a drive-through or a twelve-dollar parking charge to reach a sit-down place. The crew parks on a posted schedule, keeps the menu tight, and moves when the neighborhoods need them to.

, Owner

Book Jolly Frog LLC.

We started Jolly Frog because Madison has plenty of restaurants with long waits and parking headaches built around them, and we thought the city deserved a cleaner lunch option. The truck is a simpler idea: park where people actually are, serve a tight menu done well, and run on a posted schedule instead of a host-stand reservation list. We have a growing number of regulars across Middleton, Fitchburg, and the east side who know where we park on Tuesday, and that is the whole model.
