The plan list

How we run yards in Ocala

  • Weekly mow + edge package
    Cut at the right height for St. Augustine or Bahia, edge walks and drive, trim fence lines, full blow off.
  • Bi-weekly trim package
    Same scope as weekly on a 14 day rotation. Best for shaded yards and the slow growth months.
  • Mulch refresh
    Re-edge bed lines clean, pull weeds by hand, lay fresh pine bark or cypress at two inches across the beds.
  • Overgrown yard reset
    Knock down tall growth in passes, bag the heavy clippings, edge everything back to clean lines, haul off the debris.
  • Leaf and storm cleanup
    Rake and blow leaves from beds and lawn, clear branches after a blow through, bag and haul off.
