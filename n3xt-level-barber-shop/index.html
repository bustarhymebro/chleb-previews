Tape-up + enhancement
Sharp tape line at the hairline, light fiber fill on thinning spots, blow-dried and brushed before you leave the chair.

Senior cut + tidy
Scissor cut over comb, ear and brow trim, neck cleaned with the straight razor. Quiet chair, no upsell.
