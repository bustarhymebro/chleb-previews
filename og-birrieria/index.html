OG Birrieria at 390 S 1st Ave in Hillsboro serves birria in every form, from the classic taco plate to ramen and pizza, seven days a week.

Why Hillsboro calls OG Birrieria the best

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Parked at 390 S 1st Ave in Downtown Hillsboro, open seven days a week starting at 10 AM weekends.
Order at the window
Choose your form: taco plate, ramen, pizza, burrito, or quesadilla, each made to order on the spot.
Get the consomme
Every order comes with house consomme on the side for dipping; that part of the meal is non-negotiable.

The list

What we make.

OG Taco Plate

Four birria tacos, slow-braised beef, house consomme on the side for dipping. The one that started everything.

Birria ramen

Ramen noodles in house birria consomme broth, loaded with braised beef. Not on most menus in the PNW.

Birria pizza

Pizza crust topped with braised birria beef and melted cheese, built to order at the window.

Birria burrito with mozzarella

Full-size burrito, birria beef and mozzarella, rolled tight. Pull the end and dip it in consomme.

Quesadilla de birria

Folded, pressed quesadilla with birria beef and melted cheese, served with consomme on the side.

A short note

From Owner.

OG Birrieria started in Hillsboro with one standard: portions that never skimp on the meat. The menu grew to cover birria in forms most PNW trucks never attempt, from ramen to pizza, all braised the same way from the same recipe. If you have had birria anywhere else in the Pacific Northwest and think you found the best, come to 390 S 1st Ave and settle it.

, Owner
