El Guero's Food Truck

Run by Derekina · Roanoke

El Guero's runs a char-grill out of a truck on Williamson Road NW in Roanoke, serving tacos, burritos, and birria quesadillas to a regular lunch crowd.

Why Roanoke eats with us

What happens when you call.

We post the day's location and hours on Facebook and Instagram. Usually 4331 Williamson Rd NW.

Tacos, burritos, quesadillas, birria, ribs, whole chicken, agua de melón or piña.
Eat it fresh
Everything comes off the char grill, gets dressed, and hits your hand in a few minutes.

The list

What we make.

Char-grilled meat tacos

Three corn tortillas, char-grilled meat of choice, topped with fresh cilantro, onion, radish, and cucumbers. Salsa on the side.

Birria quesadillas

Slow-cooked beef birria pressed into a griddled flour tortilla with melted cheese, served with a cup of consomé for dipping.

Burritos and quesadillas

Flour tortilla, char-grilled meat, rice, beans, cheese, salsa. Quesadillas the same build without the rice and beans.

Whole chicken plate

Whole char-grilled chicken with house salsa, charro beans, and a stack of tortillas. Built to feed a family.

Ribs and charro beans

Char-grilled ribs over a bowl of charro beans simmered with bacon and chiles. Eat it with a tortilla and a squeeze of lime.

Book El Guero's Food Truck.

We started El Guero's to bring real char-grilled Mexican food to Roanoke, the kind cooked over fire instead of under a heat lamp. You will find the truck on Williamson Road most days, with a line that moves fast and a menu that does not pretend to be anything other than what it is. Tacos, burritos, quesadillas, birria, ribs, and a cold agua to wash it down.
