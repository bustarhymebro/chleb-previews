The menu

How Pinellas Park gets a haircut

Haircuts
Standard men's and boys' cuts. Scissor or clipper, your call. In and out without a wait list or an app.
Fades
Skin to taper, blended into whatever length you keep on top. Cleaned up around the ears and neck before you leave the chair.
Beard trim
Line the cheeks, shape the jaw, trim to length. Pairs with any haircut or booked solo.
Shave
Full straight-razor shave with hot lather and hot towels. Old-school finish, no shortcuts.
Military haircut
High and tight, regulation length, done right the first time. Active duty and veterans always welcome at the chair.
