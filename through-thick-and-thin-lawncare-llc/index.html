The plan list

How we run a route in Fayetteville

  • Weekly mow + edge package
    Mow, string trim, hard edge along concrete, blow off drive and walk. Same crew, same day each week.
  • Fall leaf cleanup
    Full leaf haul off lawn and beds, gutter line cleared at ground level, debris hauled off site, not bagged at the curb.
  • Bermuda scalp + reset
    Low cut in late March to wake the lawn up, clippings collected, edges re-cut, ready for the first green up.
  • Mulch refresh
    Bed edge re-cut by hand, two inches of fresh hardwood or pine straw, no plastic edging, no dyed black mulch.
  • One time cleanup
    For yards that got away. We mow, edge, trim back the overgrowth, and quote a weekly rate once it is back in shape.
