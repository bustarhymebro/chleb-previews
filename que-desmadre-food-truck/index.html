The list

What we make.

Mexico City style tacos

Two corn tortillas, plancha-seared meat, onion and cilantro, salsa verde or roja. Built the way we eat them back home.

Pambazos

Bread dipped in guajillo, filled with papa con chorizo, lettuce, queso fresco, crema. Pressed until the outside crackles.

Chicken tinga tacos

Shredded chicken braised in chipotle and tomato, my mother's recipe. Goes on the tortilla with onion, cilantro, a wedge of lime.

Salsa and cilantro crema

Salsas built fresh in the truck. Cilantro crema for the pambazos and anything else you want to drown.

Event catering

We quote per head, set a menu, and bring the truck or a station. Tacos, pambazos, sides, the full Que Desmadre run.

A short note

From Javier.

Javier came up from Mexico City at 17 and ran kitchens around Denver before opening Que Desmadre in 2020, three months before the city shut down. He kept the truck running through it, added a second one, Mas Chingon, and still cooks his mother's tinga every week.

— Javier
