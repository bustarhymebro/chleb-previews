How the truck runs in Land O Lakes

What happens when you call.

Check the route
We post the week's stops every Sunday night. Pick the one closest to you.
Roll up
Order at the window. Most tickets are out in under ten minutes during a normal lunch.
Eat
Grab a spot on the curb or take it back to the office. Trays come back, food doesn't.

The list

What we make.

Smash burger basket

Two thin patties on a buttered bun, american cheese, pickles, house sauce. Comes with seasoned fries and a drink.

Birria tacos

Three tacos on corn, dipped and crisped on the flat top, cilantro and onion, cup of consomme on the side.

Loaded fries

Seasoned fries under chopped brisket or chicken, cheese sauce, pickled onion, drizzle of house sauce. Built to share or not.

Chicken sandwich

Buttermilk brined thigh, fried crisp, slaw, pickles, soft potato bun. Plain or tossed in the hot honey on the way out.

Daily special

One off-menu item per service. Posted on the truck's window when we open. When it sells out, it's gone for the day.

A short note

From Owner.

Route 787 runs out of one truck and one kitchen. The owner started it after years of cooking indoors, wanting a route that actually showed up where people lived. The truck is on the road most weeks, the menu stays tight on purpose, and the phone gets answered by the person cooking your food.

— Owner

Book Route 787.

We started Route 787 because every food truck in this stretch of Pasco County seems to run on a maybe-we'll-be-there schedule and a voicemail box. We post the week, we show up, we cook until the line clears. Land O Lakes is the home base and the route works outward from there.
