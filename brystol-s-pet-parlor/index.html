About

How we run grooms in Terre Haute

We started Brystol's because most groomers in Terre Haute either book out for weeks or don't answer the phone at all. We run one dog at a time on the table, no stacking six appointments into an hour, so your dog isn't waiting in a crate half the day. A few hundred regulars know us for showing up on time and calling back the same day. We treat every groom like it's for our own dog.

Meet Owner

Brystol picked up grooming shears at a barn expo more than a decade ago and never put them down, opening the parlor after years of mobile grooming out of a converted trailer. Every dog gets hand-dried, not caged, and nothing gets sedated just to sit still.
