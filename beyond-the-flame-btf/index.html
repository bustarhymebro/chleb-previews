The list

What we make.

Gold wings basket

The signature. Hand-dredged wings finished in our gold sauce, served with fries and a side of ranch or comeback.

Southern cuisine plates

Rotating Southern plate of the day off the trailer window. Protein, two sides, cornbread or roll, sweet tea on request.

Catering trays

Half and full pans of wings, sides, and mains for groups of fifteen and up. Pickup or drop-off in Montgomery.

Event and festival vending

Full trailer service for festivals, family days, and corporate events. Booked by date, deposit holds the slot.

Private party package

Trailer pulls up to your venue, runs a set menu for your guest count, and serves off the window for two to three hours.
