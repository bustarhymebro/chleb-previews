Beyond The Flame BTF rolls a small wood-fired kitchen to lots, parks, and private bookings around Montgomery, cooked to order, served hot off the line.

How we run the truck in Montgomery

What happens when you call.

Check the post
We drop the day's lot and hours on the page before nine each morning.
Pull up
Walk to the window, order off the chalkboard, cards and cash both work.
Eat hot
Plates come straight off the line, sauce and bread on the tray, find a seat.

The list

What we make.

Brisket plate

Quarter pound of pecan-smoked brisket, two sides, white bread, pickles and onion on the tray.

Burnt ends and rib combo

Three St. Louis ribs and a scoop of burnt ends, sauce on the side, your pick of one side.

Smoked wing basket

Six jumbo wings smoked then crisped on the flat top, tossed in house rub or Alabama white.

Pulled pork sandwich

Hand-pulled shoulder on a brioche bun with slaw and pickle, served with chips or a side swap.

Family pack

One pound brisket, one pound pork, six ribs, two pints of sides, eight rolls. Feeds four hungry adults.

A short note

From Owner.

The Owner has been running pits in central Alabama for years before the truck rolled out. Started Beyond The Flame because the good lots in Montgomery never had real wood-smoked food parked on them, only fryer trucks. Answers the booking line directly.

— Owner

Book Beyond The Flame BTF.

We started Beyond The Flame because every barbecue spot worth eating at in Montgomery runs on a one-hour wait and a parking lot full of lunch hour. So we brought the pit to you. We post the lot the night before, fire pecan and hickory before sunrise, and serve until the pans run out. When the pans are gone, we are gone.
