Why Garland keeps coming back for birria

What happens when you call.

Pull up
Find the truck on S Garland Ave or wherever the week's event takes us.
Order at the window
Birria, quesabirria, or whatever's on the board that day, cash or card.
Dunk and eat
Consommé in one hand, taco in the other, sauce on the table, eat it hot.

The list

What we make.

Birria tacos

Three corn tortillas dipped in chile oil, beef braised hours in adobo, white onion, cilantro, lime, consommé on the side.

Quesabirria

Same braise pressed into a quesadilla with melted cheese, griddled until the cheese goes crisp around the edge.

Mexican food and tacos

Rotating tacos beyond the birria board, asada and pastor when the truck is set up for a long service.

Takeaway

Walk-up orders at 3335 S Garland Ave, packed for the car ride, consommé sealed separate from the tacos.

Catering and events

Trunk or Treats, school nights, private parties around Garland and Rockwall. We bring the plancha and the pot.

A short note

From Owner.

La TacoTrona has been running the birria pot on S Garland Ave long enough to land in the top ten Mexican spots in Garland. The braise goes in early, the consommé reduces all day, and nothing leaves the window until the tortilla is crisp on both sides.

— Owner
