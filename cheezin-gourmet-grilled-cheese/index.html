The list

What we make.

Mac Attack grilled cheese

House mac and cheese pressed between two slices of buttered sourdough with a sharp cheddar blend, griddled until the crust crackles.

Buffalo Chicken grilled cheese

Pulled buffalo chicken, melted provolone, and a drizzle of ranch on griddled Texas toast. Heat dialed to medium unless you ask otherwise.

Pesto Chicken Melt

Grilled chicken, basil pesto, mozzarella, and roasted tomato on sourdough. The one regulars order when they want lunch to feel like dinner.

Philly cheesesteak

Shaved ribeye, grilled onions and peppers, white American melted over the top on a toasted hoagie roll. Wrapped in foil, ready to walk.

Steak plate

Hand-cut steak griddled to temp with a side, plated off the truck. Limited quantity each service, first come at the window.
