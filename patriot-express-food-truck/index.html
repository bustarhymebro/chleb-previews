Book Patriot Express Food Truck.

We started Patriot Express because lunch in the Treasure Valley too often meant a drive-through line or a sit-down that burns an hour of the workday. Nampa has people who work hard and eat at noon, and we wanted a truck that showed up to the job site and the park with food worth the stop. Every plate is cooked to order, every stop is on a real schedule, and we pick up the phone when someone wants to book the truck.
