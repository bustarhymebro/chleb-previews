The plan list

How we run yards around Athens

  • Mowing
    Cut, string-trim around beds and fence lines, edge concrete, blow off the drive and walks. Weekly or biweekly.
  • Mulch application
    Light bed cleanup, hand-edge the beds, install hardwood or dyed mulch at consistent depth. Estimate before we start.
  • Pine straw installation
    Fresh long-needle straw spread by hand around trees, shrubs, and beds. Tucked at the edges so the wind doesn't lift it.
  • Fall cleanup
    Full leaf removal off lawn and beds, gutter line cleared, hauled off or curbed for county pickup. One visit or scheduled.
  • Aeration and fertilization
    Core aerate compacted Bermuda or fescue, follow with the right seasonal feed and pre-emergent. Best in fall and early spring.
  • Sprinkler system repair
    Track the zone, dig the head or line, replace and reset coverage. Quick fixes on broken heads and stuck valves.
