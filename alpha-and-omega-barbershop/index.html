Adult haircut
Wash, cut, style. Fifteen dollars flat, includes the neckline and a quick brush down before you leave.

Skin, taper, or scissor fade with a crisp lineup at the hairline. Most-requested combo in the shop.
Beard trim
Cheek line, neck line, scissor-over-comb on the length, hot towel to finish. Add to any cut.

Thirteen dollars, twelve and under. Booster seat available, no rush, parents welcome to sit in.

Eyebrow cleanup with wax or razor, plus eyelash service on request. Quick add-on at the end of a cut.
