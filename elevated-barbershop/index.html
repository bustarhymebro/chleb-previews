How we run the chair in Corpus Christi

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Fade
Skin, low, mid, or high. Tight blend from the guard down, scissor work on top, neck cleaned with a straight razor.
Taper
Clean taper around the ears and nape that keeps your length on top. Best every couple weeks between full cuts.
Beard trim
Shape, edge, and line along the cheek and jaw. Hot towel finish with beard oil worked through so it sits flat.
Line-up
Sharp edges on the hairline, sideburns, and nape with the trimmer and straight razor. Walk-in friendly, about ten minutes.
Hot towel shave
Full straight-razor shave with two hot towels, pre-shave oil, lather, and aftershave balm. Around forty-five minutes in the chair.
