Ceramic coating

Decon wash, iron and tar removal, one-step polish, panel wipe, then a multi-year ceramic on paint, wheels, and glass.

Multi-stage paint correction

Two or three stage compound and polish to remove deeper defects. Includes paint depth readings and a 50/50 reveal before final coating.

6-month sealant application

Entry-level protection for daily drivers. Hand wash, clay decon, light polish, then a polymer sealant rated for roughly six months.

Interior leather restoration

Deep clean of seats and trim, conditioner massaged in, color-match dye on worn bolsters, UV protectant to slow the next round of fade.

Auto detailing

Collection maintenance

Standing appointments for garaged cars. Soft wash, blow-dry, ceramic-safe sealant top-up, photo log of any new chips or marks.
