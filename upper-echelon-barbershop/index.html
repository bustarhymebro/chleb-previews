The menu

How the chair runs in Largo

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Skin fade + beard line-up
Bald fade up the sides, scissor work on top, beard outlined with the straight razor. Hot towel and aftershave to close.
Taper + scissor top
Low or mid taper at the temple and nape, length kept on top, side part or loose texture depending on how it falls.
Executive trim
Quick clean-up between full cuts. Sides tightened, neck shaved, ears cleared. In and out in about twenty minutes.
Straight razor shave
Pre-shave oil, hot lather, two passes with the straight, cold towel and balm. About forty-five minutes in the chair.
Kid's cut (under 12)
Booster seat if needed, cartoon on the phone if needed. Clean cut without rushing them, no surprise clipper buzz on the neck.
