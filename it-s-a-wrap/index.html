The list

What we make.

Blackened chicken Caesar wrap

Cast-iron blackened thigh, romaine, shaved parm, lemon Caesar, pulled in a toasted spinach tortilla.

Brisket chopped salad

Smoked brisket trim, romaine, pickled onion, sharp cheddar, crispy tortilla, chipotle ranch tossed at the window.

Gulf shrimp rice bowl

Seared Gulf shrimp over cilantro lime rice, black beans, pico, avocado crema, pickled jalapeno on top.

Buffalo cauliflower wrap

Roasted cauliflower tossed in buffalo, slaw, blue cheese crumble, ranch drizzle, wrapped in a jalapeno cheddar tortilla.

Steak chimichurri bowl

Sliced skirt steak over arugula and rice, charred corn, cotija, chimichurri spooned over the top.
