The list

What we make.

Gourmet street burger

Hand-pattied beef, melted cheese, house sauce on a soft bun. The burger that won Best of Toledo in 2021, 2023, and 2024.

Hand-pattied double

Two fresh-ground patties, smashed and stacked, with your pick from the toppings rail. Built to order on the window.

Fresh cut fries basket

Potatoes cut in the truck that morning, fried golden, salted hot. Side or full basket.

Burger and fries combo

One gourmet street burger, a basket of fresh cut fries, a drink. The order most regulars walk up and point at.

Event and festival catering

Truck on site for weddings, corporate lots, and Northwest Ohio festivals. Custom counts and a set menu agreed up front.

A short note

From Owner.

Adam Brandt has been running The Beastro since the early days of the Toledo food truck scene, one of the first independent operators in town. He still hand-patties the burgers, picks up his own phone for bookings, and the Best of Toledo wins in 2021, 2023, and 2024 are why people chase the truck around Northwest Ohio.
