How the truck runs in Tampa

What happens when you call.

Check the schedule
We post the week's stops every Sunday night. Pick the day and location that works for you.
Order at the window
Walk up, order off the board, pay card or cash. Most plates are out in under ten minutes.
Eat where you stand
Grab a seat near the truck, dress your bowl, and head back to the rest of your day.

The list

What we make.

Pho bo

Beef broth simmered overnight with charred onion and star anise, rice noodles, thin sliced eye round, herbs on the side.

Banh mi thit nuong

Grilled lemongrass pork on a toasted baguette, pickled daikon and carrot, cucumber, cilantro, jalapeno, house mayo.

Com tam suon

Broken rice plate with grilled pork chop, scallion oil, pickles, cucumber, fried egg on request, nuoc cham on the side.

Bun thit nuong

Cold rice vermicelli bowl, grilled pork, lettuce, herbs, bean sprouts, crushed peanuts, crispy shallots, nuoc cham to dress.

Goi cuon

Fresh rice paper rolls with shrimp, pork, vermicelli, mint, and lettuce. Served cold with peanut hoisin dipping sauce.
