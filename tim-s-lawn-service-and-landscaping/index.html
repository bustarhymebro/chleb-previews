The plan list

How we run routes in Palm Bay

  • Weekly mow + edge package
    Cut, edge, line-trim, and blow off. Bagged or mulched. Billed monthly, four visits in growing season.
  • Bi-weekly cool-season plan
    November through February schedule for slower growth. Same crew, just on a two-week rotation to match the season.
  • Mulch refresh by the yard
    Bed edge re-cut, weeds pulled, two to three inches of fresh mulch laid. Pine bark, cypress, or red.
  • Palm trim and clean-up
    Clear boots and seed pods on queen, sabal, and washingtonia palms up to about twenty feet. Haul-off included.
  • One-time clean-up
    Overgrown yards reset to a maintainable baseline. Cut down, edge, trim beds, haul debris, ready for a weekly route.
