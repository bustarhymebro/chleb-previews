The list

What we make.

Shrimp grilled cheese

Buttered Texas toast, seasoned shrimp, melted cheese pulled long. The plate people text their coworkers about.

Cheesesteak fries

Hand-cut fries loaded with chopped steak, peppers, onions, and cheese sauce poured over the top right at the window.

Pizza fries

Crispy fries layered with marinara, mozzarella, pepperoni, and Italian seasoning. Knife and fork strongly suggested.

Chicken and waffles

Golden waffle, hand-breaded chicken, syrup and hot sauce on the side. Breakfast plate that works any hour.

Polish dogs and wings

Snap-casing polish dog with the trimmings, plus wings tossed in your sauce. Sliders and veggie plates also on the board.
