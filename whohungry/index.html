WhoHungry?

Run by Shun · Montgomery

Shun's truck rolls Montgomery weekly with hot plates off the smoker, built for lunch crews, block parties, and folks who already know the menu.

How the truck runs in Montgomery

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Check the week's stops by phone or text, we post the schedule every Sunday night.
Order at the window
Pick a plate, pay card or cash, we hand you a number and a tea.
Eat hot
Tickets run about ten minutes. Sauces and napkins on the side rail by the window.

The list

What we make.

Smoked wing plate

Six smoked wings tossed in house sauce, two sides, slice of Texas toast. Comes with sweet tea by default.

Brisket loaded fries

Hand-cut fries, chopped brisket, cheese sauce, pickled onion, drizzle of the sweet-heat. Built for one, eats like two.

Rib tip basket

Half-pound of smoked rib tips, white bread, slaw, sauce on the side. Friday and Saturday only, while the pan lasts.

Catfish plate

Two fillets fried to order, hush puppies, slaw, lemon. Hot sauce on the window, tartar in the bag.

Fish and shrimp combo

One fillet plus six fried shrimp over fries, cocktail and tartar tucked in the box. Family favorite at private bookings.

A short note

From Shun.

Shun has been cooking since the back-of-the-house days at family cookouts in Montgomery, and started the truck after years of friends asking him to plate up for their parties. He answers his own phone, runs the smoker himself, and won't sell a plate he wouldn't feed his own table.

— Shun
