WhoHungry? is Shun's food truck rolling through Montgomery with a tight menu, fair prices, and a line that moves while the music plays.

How the truck runs in Montgomery

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Check the location post the night before, pull up to the window during service hours.
Order at the window
Tell us what you want, grab a number, card or cash both work fine.
Eat hot
Food comes up in under ten minutes, fresh off the flat top, never sitting under a lamp.

The list

What we make.

Loaded smash burger

Two smashed patties, american, grilled onions, house sauce, toasted brioche. Comes with seasoned fries and a pickle spear.

Wing basket

Six or ten wings tossed in lemon pepper, hot honey, or garlic parmesan. Fries underneath, ranch or blue cheese on the side.

Philly cheesesteak

Shaved ribeye, peppers, onions, melted provolone on a hoagie roll. Served with chips or upgrade to fries for two dollars.

Loaded fry plate

Fries piled with cheese, bacon, sour cream, and choice of chopped chicken or steak. Plate big enough to share, most folks don't.

Shrimp basket

Hand-breaded gulf shrimp fried to order, fries, hush puppies, cocktail and tartar on the side. Lemon wedge on top.

A short note

From Shun.

Shun started WhoHungry? in Montgomery because the food he wanted to eat after a long shift wasn't on any menu in town. He runs the window himself most days, preps the sauces in-house, and posts the truck's spot the night before so regulars know where to find him.

— Shun
