About

How we run the truck in Jackson

We started Taqueria La Reata to bring Jackson the kind of street tacos you actually find in Mexico, not the version you get at a chain. Every truck in town runs on weather, word of mouth, and a phone that rings off the hook at lunch, so we keep the menu tight and the line moving. The meats are marinated the night before, the tortillas are pressed fresh, and the salsas are made in house. Pull up hungry.

Meet Owner

The owner has been cooking these recipes since long before the truck had wheels under it, working off the same family playbook out of central Mexico. They prep the meats themselves every morning, answer the phone for catering, and won't run pastor that wasn't marinated overnight.
