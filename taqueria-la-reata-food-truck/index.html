The list

What we make.

Tacos al pastor

Marinated pork off the trompo, pineapple, onion, cilantro, two corn tortillas, salsa verde on the side.

Birria quesatacos

Slow-braised beef, melted Oaxaca cheese, griddled tortilla, consomé cup for dipping. Three per order.

Carne asada plate

Grilled skirt steak, rice, charro beans, grilled onion and jalapeño, four tortillas, lime.

Pollo asado burrito

Marinated chicken thigh, rice, beans, cheese, pico, salsa roja, wrapped in a flour tortilla and pressed.

Elote and agua fresca

Grilled corn with crema, cotija, chile and lime. Horchata or jamaica by the cup or gallon.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner has been cooking these recipes since long before the truck. Family started in the kitchen, moved to the window when the lunch line outgrew the dining room. Tortillas pressed daily, trompo cut to order, no shortcuts on the salsas.

— Owner
