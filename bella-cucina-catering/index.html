The list

What we make.

Housemade pasta station

Fresh-rolled fettuccine or penne, choice of marinara, alfredo, or bolognese, served plated or buffet-style.

Bruschetta + antipasto board

Grilled sourdough, marinated olives, fresh mozzarella, roasted peppers, and cured meats. Priced per guest.

Chicken marsala platter

Pan-finished in a mushroom and dry marsala reduction, served over egg noodles or alongside roasted potatoes.

House tiramisu and ricotta-filled cannoli, portioned by the dozen. Add-on to any catering package.

Build-your-own risotto bar

Arborio base finished on site, toppings line of pancetta, parmesan, roasted mushrooms, and fresh herbs.
