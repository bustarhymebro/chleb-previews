What we make.

Smash burger + fries basket

Two seared smash patties, american, grilled onion, house sauce on a brioche bun, with a basket of seasoned fries.

Nashville hot chicken sandwich

Buttermilk-brined thigh, fried, dipped in cayenne oil, dill pickles and slaw on a toasted bun.

Brisket + cheddar quesadilla

Twelve-hour smoked brisket, sharp cheddar, pickled red onion, pressed on the flat top, served with chipotle crema.

Loaded street fries

Fresh-cut fries under brisket or chicken, queso, pickled jalapeño, scallion, crema. Built to share, eaten with a fork.

Event taco bar

Three proteins, warm flour and corn tortillas, four salsas, rice, beans. Priced per head, served off the truck.
