Get Loaded rolls a small kitchen into Huntsville lots and lunch rushes, cooking to order with a short menu and a fair line.

How the truck runs in Huntsville

What happens when you call.

Check the stop
We post the day's location and window the night before on the truck page.
Order at the window
Walk up, order off the board, pay card or cash, we hand you a number.
Built to order
Food hits the flat-top after you order, comes out hot in five to eight minutes.

The list

What we make.

Loaded smash burger

Two smashed patties, american, grilled onion, house sauce, brioche bun. Served with a fork because it earns it.

Brisket loaded fries

Hand-cut fries, chopped brisket, queso, pickled jalapeno, scallion. Comes in a boat, not a basket.

Nashville hot chicken sandwich

Buttermilk thigh, dipped to your heat, slaw, pickles, soft roll. Mild, hot, or the one we warn you about.

Chopped cheese

Seasoned ground beef, onion, melted american, lettuce, tomato on a hoagie. Built on the flat-top, wrapped in foil.

Truck-style street tacos

Three corn tortillas, choice of brisket or chicken, onion, cilantro, salsa verde. Lime wedge on the side.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner started Get Loaded after years of cooking line in Huntsville kitchens and getting tired of watching good food die under a heat lamp. They run the truck, answer the phone, and post the next stop themselves. No franchise, no ghost kitchen.

— Owner
