How the truck runs in Huntsville

What happens when you call.

Check the schedule
We post the week's stops on the page Sunday night so you can plan your lunch around the truck.
Pull up to the window
Walk up, order off the board, or call ahead for pickup when the lot gets deep at noon.
Eat it hot
Plates come out fast off the flat top. Tables when we have them, tailgates when we don't.

The list

What we make.

Loaded Fries

Hand-cut fries piled with cheese, your choice of pulled pork or chicken, scallions, and the house drizzle. The plate Mickey built the truck on.

Loaded Potatoes

Whole baked russet split open and loaded with pork or chicken, cheese, butter, and sour cream. Heavy enough to be the whole lunch.

Cheesy Mac

Elbow macaroni in the house cheese sauce, served as a side or piled into a bowl with pulled meat and a crispy top.

Crispy Nachos

Fresh-fried chips, cheese, pulled pork or chicken, jalapenos, and the works. Made to share or finish alone, no judgment.

Brunswick Stew

Slow-cooked stew with pork, chicken, corn, and tomato. Cup or bowl. The cold-weather pull on the menu when the truck runs late fall.

A short note

From Mickey.

Mickey Williams has been in food service since he was a teenager and started Get Loaded in 2019 to put real loaded plates in front of working Huntsville. Seven years in, he still answers the phone, runs the window, and writes the catering quotes himself.

— Mickey

Book Get Loaded.

We started Get Loaded in 2019 out of the kitchen at 1001 Ryland Pike because every food truck in town was chasing the same three sandwiches and nobody was doing loaded plates right. Seven years later the menu still lives or dies on the fries, the stew, and the cheese sauce. Mickey runs the window, the truck, and the books.
