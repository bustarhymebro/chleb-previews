Why Pikesville eats at Mangal Grill

A short note

From Owner.

The owner started Mangal Grill Express to give Baltimore's kosher community a real Israeli street food option under full Star-K supervision. The rotisserie runs every day the truck is open, and the shawarma is carved fresh for each order, not sitting in a steam tray. Sunday through Thursday on Reisterstown Road in Pikesville, closed every Shabbat.

— Owner
