The plan list

How we run lawns in Newnan

  • Weekly mow and edge package
    Cut, string trim, hard edge on concrete, blow off drive, walks, and porch. Same day every week, same crew.
  • Bi-weekly maintenance plan
    Every other week mow with edge and blow, bed touch-up once a month. Built for shaded yards and slower growth.
  • Spring cleanup and mulch refresh
    Bed cleanup, pre-emergent down, fresh hardwood or pine mulch installed at two inches across all front beds.
  • Fall leaf removal
    Full property blow down, tarp haul to the curb or trailer, gutter clear add-on if leaves are still falling.
  • Aeration and overseed
    Core aeration in early fall for Fescue lawns, overseed with a sun and shade blend, starter fertilizer on top.
