The Materials

Four stones. One standard.

Compare real slabs side by side in our Hawthorne shop. We help you weigh look, durability and care so the stone fits the way you actually live.

Granite

Natural stone with one of a kind patterns and serious heat resistance. Over 300 varieties to choose from, sourced from quarries worldwide.

Typical installed range$45 to $100 per sq ft

Marble

Classic veining for baths, fireplace surrounds and statement kitchens. Timeless, elegant, and unlike anything engineered.

Typical installed range$60 to $150 per sq ft

Quartz

Non-porous, consistent color, and never needs sealing. The most popular pick for busy kitchens. Silestone, Cambria and Caesarstone.

Typical installed range$50 to $120 per sq ft

Porcelain

Extremely durable, heat and scratch resistant, beautiful indoors and out. Includes Dekton ultracompact surfaces for modern looks.

Typical installed range$55 to $120 per sq ft

How It Works

A clear path from slab to install.

01

Consult & Template

Visit the shop, compare real slabs with a personalized design consultation, and get a free no-obligation quote. We template your space precisely so every cut is exact.

02

Fabrication

Your tops are cut and finished in-house on laser and digital stone fabrication equipment. Many jobs turn around in as little as 3 to 4 business days.

03

Installation

Most projects install about one to two weeks after templating, and the install itself usually takes a few hours to one day. Tear-out of your old tops can be part of the job.

Your kitchen is not torn apart for weeks. It is measured once, fabricated fast, and installed in a single visit on most projects.
