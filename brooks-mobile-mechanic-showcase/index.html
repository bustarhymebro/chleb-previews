Behind the van

Dennis runs the truck.

I started Brooks Mobile Mechanic because every shop in Fayetteville quoted a three-week wait for jobs that take ninety minutes once you've got the parts. The van fixes that. I bring the parts, I bring the lift, I bring the diagnostic. The truck stays in your driveway.

Fifteen years across NW Arkansas before this, mostly fleet and dealership service drives. The thing both taught me: the customer wants the truck running, today, in their lot. Everything else is a story dealers tell to fill bays.

I won't take a job I can't finish that day. If you call and I'm booked, I'll tell you that on the phone — not after the diagnostic.
