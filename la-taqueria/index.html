The list

What we make.

Authentic Mexican street tacos

Corn tortilla, choice of meat, white onion, cilantro, lime wedge. Salsa roja or verde on the side.

Fruit creations (La Fruteria)

Fresh mango, watermelon, pineapple, cucumber. Tajín, chamoy, lime on request. Built in the cup to order.

Mangonada

Mango sorbet, fresh mango chunks, chamoy, Tajín, tamarind straw. The reason regulars walk up in August.

Agua fresca of the day

Horchata, jamaica, or whatever fruit came in fresh that morning. Poured cold from the jar at the window.

Taco plate

Three street tacos, choice of meat, rice and beans on the side. The standard lunch order from regulars.
