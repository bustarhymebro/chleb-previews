Services

How West Tampa books Gold Cut

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Men's haircut
Consult, cut, fade or scissor work, hot towel, neck shave, and styling. Standard chair time, no upcharge surprises.
Beard trim and styling
Outline, shape, and trim with razor detail. Beard oil finish. Pairs with any haircut on the same visit.
Hot lather shave
Full straight razor shave, hot lather, two-towel prep, aftershave balm. Roughly thirty minutes in the chair.
Hair painting and color
Freehand color and highlights done in chair. Booked ahead so we hold the time for processing and rinse.
Hair designs
Razor designs and parts done after the cut. Bring a reference photo or talk it through with the barber first.
