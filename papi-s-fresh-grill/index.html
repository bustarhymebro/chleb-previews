How the truck runs in Lakeland

What happens when you call.

Check the post
We drop the next day's location and hours on our page the evening before service.
Roll up
Order at the window. Plates come up hot within ten minutes on a normal lunch rush.
Eat fresh
Everything is grilled to order on the flat-top. No heat lamps, no holding trays.

The list

What we make.

Grilled chicken plate

Marinated chicken off the flat-top, yellow rice, black beans, and a side of plantains. House green sauce on request.

Carne asada plate

Sliced skirt steak grilled to order, rice, beans, pico, and a warm tortilla stack. Add cheese or avocado.

Loaded fries

Hand-cut fries under chopped grilled protein, melted cheese, pico, and crema. Built like a meal, not a side.

Tacos, three count

Three soft corn tacos with your choice of grilled protein, onion, cilantro, lime. Salsa roja or verde on the side.

Family meal box

Two proteins, double rice, double beans, tortillas, and salsas. Feeds four hungry adults. Pickup or delivery within Lakeland.

A short note

From Owner.

Papi's Fresh Grill is run out of one truck, one flat-top, and one family. The owner takes the orders, fires the grill, and posts where the truck will be the next day. We do not freeze proteins, and we do not run a second truck we cannot stand behind.

— Owner

Book Papi's Fresh Grill.

We started Papi's Fresh Grill because Lakeland needed a grill truck that actually cooks the plate when you order it. Every shop in town runs the same frozen patties and the same heat-lamp rice, and we got tired of eating it. We park, we grill, we feed the lunch crowd, and we post tomorrow's spot before we close out the night.
