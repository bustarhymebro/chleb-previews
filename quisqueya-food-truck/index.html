The list

What we make.

Mofongo

Mashed green plantain with garlic and chicharron, served with your choice of stewed chicken, pernil, or shrimp on the side.

Pernil plate

Slow-roasted pork shoulder, rice and beans, sweet plantains. Cut to order off the pan at the window.

Chicharron de pollo

Crispy fried chicken pieces marinated Dominican style, served with rice, beans, and a side of pink sauce.

Late-night combo

Pick a protein, pick two sides, eat it on the curb. The plate the after-midnight regulars order without looking at a menu.

Empanadas

Hand-folded beef or chicken empanadas, fried to order. The quick bite for the line that builds after the bars let out.

A short note

From Owner.

We park Quisqueya at 17th and Tilghman four nights a week because Allentown deserves a real Dominican plate at 2 AM, not a microwave burrito. Same family in the truck every night, same recipes from back home, same window from sundown to almost 3.

Book Quisqueya Food Truck.

We started Quisqueya because the late shift in Allentown had nowhere to eat real food after the kitchens closed. Every night we park at 17th and Tilghman, fire up the plancha, and cook Dominican plates the way our family taught us. If the lights are on, the food is hot, and the window is open.
