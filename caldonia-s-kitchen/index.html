What we're known for

Specialties

Jerk chicken plate
Quarter chicken marinated overnight, grilled over pimento wood, served with rice and peas, cabbage, and fried plantains.
Oxtail and butter beans
Braised low until the meat slides off the bone, finished with butter beans, scotch bonnet, and a scoop of white rice.
Curry goat
Bone-in goat slow-cooked in island curry with potato and onion. Served with rice and peas and steamed cabbage.
Brown stew snapper
Whole snapper fried then simmered in brown stew gravy with peppers and onions. Available Friday and Saturday only.
Festival and plantains
House-made cornmeal festival dumplings fried to order, plus sweet plantains. Add to any plate or take a side order.

About

How we run the truck in Orlando

We started Caldonia's Kitchen because Orlando deserves Caribbean food cooked the way it gets cooked at home, not rushed under a heat lamp. Every plate goes together when you order it, the marinades sit overnight, and the oxtail takes as long as it takes. If we run out for the day, we run out, and we are back at it the next morning.

Meet Owner

The owner has been cooking Caribbean food in Orlando kitchens for years before putting Caldonia's Kitchen on wheels. Recipes come from family, prep starts before sunrise, and nothing leaves the window that the owner wouldn't feed their own kids.
