The list

What we make.

Lomo saltado plate

Wok-seared sirloin, red onion, tomato, soy and vinegar, served over white rice with a side of fries.

Salchipapas con huevo

Sliced sausage, hand-cut fries, fried egg on top, three house sauces on the side. Built to share or not.

Pollo a la brasa quarter

Marinated rotisserie chicken quarter, choice of two sides, aji verde and aji amarillo for dipping.

Arroz chaufa de pollo

Peruvian-Chinese fried rice with chicken, scallion, soy, and egg, finished hot off the wok in under five minutes.

Ceviche cup

Fresh white fish cured in lime with red onion, cilantro, and rocoto, served cold with sweet potato and corn.

A short note

From Emmanuel.

Emmanuel grew up eating his grandmother's lomo saltado in Lima and started the truck after years of cooking the same plates at home for friends in Winston-Salem. He answers the phone himself, runs the plancha himself, and won't serve a plate he wouldn't eat.

— Emmanuel

Book Lomo House.

We started Lomo House because Winston-Salem had no shortage of tacos and barbecue, but nowhere to grab a real plate of lomo saltado on a lunch break. We cook every order to the ticket, prep the sauces in-house each morning, and post the truck where the regulars can find us. If you call, you get a person, not a voicemail tree.
