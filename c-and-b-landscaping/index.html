How C&B runs jobs in West Palm Beach

  • Weekly lawn + yard maintenance
    Mow, edge, blow, and trim on a weekly or biweekly schedule; includes light debris cleanup after each visit.
  • Custom landscape design
    Site assessment, full design plan, and plant placement guide tailored to your lot's drainage and light conditions.
  • Tree service + crown work
    Pruning, shaping, and full removal for palms and hardwoods; stump grinding quoted separately on request.
  • Palm + hardwood nursery supply
    On-site stock of palm varieties, hardwood trees, and tropicals; delivery and plant install priced into the job.
  • Full landscape contracting
    New installs from grading and sod through drainage, edging, and hardscape; crew handles it start to finish.
  • Junk removal + haul-away
    Full property cleanout, green waste removal, and debris hauling after storms, renovations, or yard overhauls.
