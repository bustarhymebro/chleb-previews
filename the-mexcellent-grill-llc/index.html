A short note

From Angie.

Angie has been running street food in Grand Island for a stretch now, started the Mexcellent Grill because she wanted the kind of grilled-to-order taco that didn't come out of a steam tray. She parks the truck herself, takes the orders herself, and won't let a plate leave the window if the tortilla went cold waiting.
