What happens when you call.

The list

What we make.

Smothered pork chop plate

Two bone-in chops in onion gravy with two sides and cornbread. Served hot off the flat top.

Fried catfish plate

Cornmeal-dusted catfish fillets, hush puppies, slaw, and your choice of mac or greens.

Oxtails and rice

Slow-braised oxtails over white rice with brown gravy, collards, and cornbread. Friday and Saturday only.

Yardbird and waffles

Buttermilk fried chicken on a sweet cream waffle with hot honey or syrup. Brunch and event menu.

Family pan

Half pan of one meat, two sides, and cornbread. Feeds six to eight, ready for pickup at the truck.
