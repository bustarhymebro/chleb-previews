The menu

How we run the chair in Columbus

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Skin fade + beard line-up
Bald fade through the sides, razor line on the beard, hot towel and aftershave to close. About forty-five minutes in the chair.
Classic taper + hot towel
Tapered sides, scissor work on top, straight razor on the neck, finished with a steamed towel and a light tonic.
Beard sculpt
Full beard shape with the trimmers, cheek and neck lines set with the razor, balm worked through to finish.
Kid's cut
Patient cut for boys under twelve. We let them watch, talk them through the clippers, send them out looking sharp.
Straight razor shave
Pre-shave oil, hot towel, lather, and a single pass with the straight razor. Cold towel and balm to close.
