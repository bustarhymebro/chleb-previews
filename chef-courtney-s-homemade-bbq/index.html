The list

What we make.

Pulled pork sandwich

Hand-pulled shoulder piled on a bun, slaw on request, your pick of sauce off the forty-flavor board.

Smoked ribs

St. Louis cut, dry-rubbed and smoked over wood until the bark sets. Sold by the half or full rack.

Baked beans

Slow-cooked with brisket trim folded in. Sweet up front, a little smoke on the finish.

Potato salad

Made by hand the morning of service. Cold, creamy, mustard-forward, the way Courtney's mother taught him to make it.

Homemade BBQ sauce

Forty plus flavors on rotation, mustard-based Carolina the house pour. Bottles sold off the trailer when stock allows.

A short note

From Courtney.

Courtney Richmond runs the trailer himself. He went to culinary school, worked food-service and construction to save for the rig, and built the menu around a mustard-based Carolina sauce his mother handed down after he graduated. He answers the phone, runs the smoker, and won't send out a plate he wouldn't eat himself.

— Courtney
