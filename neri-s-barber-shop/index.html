How the shop runs in Sun City Center

Skin fade + beard line-up
Bald fade from the skin up, scissor work on top, beard squared and lined with a straight razor.
Classic taper + part
Low or mid taper, scissor top, hard part scored in with a trimmer if you want it.
Hot towel shave
Pre-shave oil, hot towel, lather, two pass straight razor, cold towel finish and aftershave balm.
Senior gentleman's cut
Scissor cut on top, clean taper on the sides, ear and neck detail, comb and finish with pomade.
Kids cut (under 12)
Quick, no fuss. We work around the wiggling and finish with the lollipop if mom says yes.
