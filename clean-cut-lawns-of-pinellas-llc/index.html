The plan list

How we run yards in Seminole

  • Weekly mow + edge package
    Cut at proper height for St. Augustine, full perimeter trim, hard edge on concrete, blower cleanup, every seven days in season.
  • Bi-weekly winter route
    Slower cadence November through February when growth slows, same crew, same day of the week, billed lower.
  • Hedge + bed refresh
    Trim shrubs to clean lines, pull weeds out of mulch beds, light pre-emergent application, haul away the clippings.
  • Storm cleanup
    Post-storm fronds, branches, and debris cleared off lawns and driveways, chipped or hauled to the county yard.
  • Sod patch + topdress
    Cut out dead spots, lay fresh St. Augustine plugs or slabs, topdress with sand, water-in instructions left at the door.
