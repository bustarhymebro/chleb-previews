The list

What we make.

Chicken wings

House-seasoned, fried crispy, tossed in your heat pick. The signature item, cooked per order.

Steaks

Cut and cooked on the flat-top, seasoned simple, plated with a side when available.

Pasta

Sauced hot to order, cream or red base. Ask what sauce is running before you step up.

Wings + steak combo

Also offered: wings and steak on one plate. Ask at the window if it is on that day.

Steak + pasta plate

Also offered: steak over pasta on select nights when both items are fully prepped and running.
