How we run the truck in Pensacola

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Check our daily location post, roll up, and order at the window. No app, no line app.
We cook to order
Tickets fire as you order. Most plates are out in six to nine minutes.
Eat, or take it back
Grab a stool, or box it up for the office. Trays and bookings get a callback within the day.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner has been cooking Vietnamese street food out of Pensacola for years, started the truck after running kitchens for other people and getting tired of cut corners. Picks up the phone, preps every morning, and posts the next stop the night before.

— Owner

Book Di Da Food Truck.

We started Di Da to put real Vietnamese street food on the curb in Pensacola, the way it gets cooked at home. Most lunch options downtown run on a wait or a chain menu, and that left a gap. We keep the menu short, prep fresh every morning, and post where the truck will be the night before so regulars can plan around us.
