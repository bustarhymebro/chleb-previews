How we run the truck in Pensacola

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Parked at 500 E Heinberg near Wall South, outside Gulf Coast Brewery, Tuesday through Sunday.
Order at the window
Call ahead if you are short on time. Otherwise step up and Nga will walk you through it.
Eat fresh
Everything is made to order. Grab a picnic table or take it back to the brewery patio.

The list

What we make.

Bánh Mì Vietnamese Sandwich

Crackly baguette, pâté, pickled daikon and carrot, cucumber, cilantro, jalapeño. Pick pork, chicken, or vegetarian. The pork is the regular.

Gỏi Cuốn Spring Rolls

Cold rice-paper rolls wrapped to order with shrimp, pork, vermicelli, lettuce, and mint. Served with house peanut sauce.

Chả Giò Eggrolls

Crispy fried egg rolls with a juicy pork and vegetable filling. Comes with nuoc cham for dipping. Three to an order.

Char Siu Rice Box

Roasted red pork over jasmine rice with pickled vegetables and a side of broth. Heavy plate, the kind that holds you until dinner.

Cà Phê Sữa Đá

Vietnamese iced coffee, slow-dripped strong and finished with sweetened condensed milk over ice. Pairs with anything off the window.

A short note

From Nga.

Nga runs the truck with her family, parked at 500 E Heinberg outside Gulf Coast Brewery. The banh mi is the one most regulars come back for, pork shoulder roasted slow with the crackly baguette and pickled vegetables. If you call ahead she will have it ready at the window.

— Nga

Book Di Da Food Truck.

We started Di Da to bring real Vietnamese street food to Pensacola, the kind Nga grew up eating and could not find around town. We park at 500 E Heinberg most days, outside Gulf Coast Brewery, and we run a tight menu so every plate that leaves the window is right. Generous portions, fresh bread, and the banh mi is the superstar.
