How we run the truck in Pensacola

What happens when you call.

We post tomorrow's location and hours on the page by eight every night.

Pull up

The list

What we make.

Smash burger combo

Two smashed patties, American, grilled onion, house sauce on a toasted bun, with seasoned fries and a drink.

Gulf shrimp basket

Local Gulf shrimp, hand-breaded, fried to order, served over fries with remoulade and a wedge of lemon.

Blackened mahi tacos

Three flour tortillas, blackened mahi, cabbage slaw, chipotle crema, pickled red onion, lime on the side.

Loaded fry box

Seasoned fries under chopped brisket, queso, jalapenos, scallions, and a drizzle of house barbecue sauce.

Family pack

Four entrees, two large sides, four drinks. Called in by ten, ready for pickup at the window by noon.
