The list

What we make.

Tacos al Pastor

Marinated pork off the trompo, chopped to order on a warm corn tortilla, onion, cilantro, lime, salsa verde or roja.

Burritos

The one the reviews keep mentioning. Flour tortilla, rice, beans, your meat, pico, cheese, wrapped tight and griddled.

Gorditas

Thick masa pocket cooked on the plancha, split and stuffed with chicken tinga or carnitas, queso fresco, crema, lettuce.

Tamales

Steamed in the husk, masa around pork in red chile or chicken in green. Limited count daily, when they're gone they're gone.

Street Corn

Elote on the cob or in a cup. Mayo, cotija, chile powder, lime. The side that ends up being the whole meal.
