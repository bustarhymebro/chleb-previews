A short note

From Camille.

Camille grew up eating crepes on weekend mornings and got tired of waiting for someone else to bring them to Chubbuck. She started the truck after years of catering events out of a borrowed kitchen, figured a flat iron and a parking spot were all she actually needed. She folds every order herself, sweet or savory, and closes up when the batter is gone.

, Camille
