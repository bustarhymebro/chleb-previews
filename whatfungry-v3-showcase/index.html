The list

What we make.

Smashed double
$11

Two patties, charred edges, American, special sauce, soft potato bun. The reason most regulars come back the day after.

Truck birria
$12

Slow-braised brisket and chuck, dipped tortillas, melted Oaxaca, consommé on the side for the dunk. Limited daily, ask if we still have it.

Pork belly bahn-mi
$10

Crispy lacquered belly, pickled daikon, jalapeno, cilantro, sriracha mayo, French roll. Spice level: enough.

Fries with the works
$7

Loaded loaded fries

A short note

From the cab of the truck.

I started Whatfungry? because every food-truck I worked for in Montgomery either ran out of food by 1 PM or padded the menu past what they could actually cook well. We do four sandwiches, one fry, and the occasional special. We sell out clean some days, and that's the trade I'd rather make than serve a tired ninth item nobody asked for.

If you've eaten with us at RSA Tower or seen the truck at a Cloverdale night and wondered if we cater, we do. Block parties, office lunches, the rehearsal-dinner crowd who wants a smashburger bar instead of a carving station. Send a date and a head count and I'll send the math back the same day.

— Shun
