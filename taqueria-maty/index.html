What we're known for

Specialties

Al pastor tacos
Marinated pork off the trompo, pineapple, onion, cilantro, double corn tortilla. Three per order with lime and salsa verde.
Birria quesatacos
Slow-braised beef, melted Oaxaca, tortilla dipped in consomé and crisped on the plancha. Served with a cup of broth.
Carnitas plate
Michoacan-style pork, rice, refried beans, four tortillas, pickled onion. Choice of red or green salsa on the side.
Elote loco
Grilled corn off the cob, mayo, cotija, tajin, lime, hot sauce. Sized as a cup, eat it with a spoon.
Family pack of ten
Ten tacos any meat, large rice, large beans, chips, and sixteen ounces of salsa. Feeds three to four hungry adults.

About

How we run the truck in Riverview

We started Taqueria Maty because Riverview kept growing and there still wasn't a spot serving the kind of tacos you'd find on a corner in Mexico. Every meat is cooked the morning of, every tortilla is pressed in the truck, and the salsas are made by hand. If we run out, we close for the day and start over tomorrow.

Meet Maty

Maty grew up cooking the recipes her mom ran out of a kitchen in Puebla, then spent years feeding crews on job sites around south Hillsborough before putting her name on a truck. She presses the tortillas, runs the trompo, and answers the catering calls herself.
