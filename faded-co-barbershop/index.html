The menu

How we run the chair in Pinellas Park

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Skin fade + beard line-up
Bald fade taken down with the foil, beard shaped with the straight razor, hot towel and oil to finish. About 45 minutes in the chair.
Classic taper + scissor top
Tapered sides blended into a scissor-cut top. Built for guys who want it sharp at work and easy on the weekend.
Hot towel straight shave
Full straight-razor shave with hot towels, pre-shave oil, and balm. Roughly 30 minutes, head or face.
Kid's cut
Cuts for boys 12 and under. Same fade work as the adult chair, just sized for a smaller seat and a shorter attention span.
The standing appointment
Same chair, same barber, same time every two or three weeks. We hold the slot so you stop thinking about it.
