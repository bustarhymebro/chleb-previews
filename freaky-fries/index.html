The list

What we make.

OG Freaky loaded

Hand-cut fries, queso, smoked brisket, pickled onion, cilantro, house garlic crema. The one we got known for.

Buffalo chicken fries

Crispy chicken, buffalo toss, blue cheese crumble, scallion, ranch drizzle on a base of seasoned fries.

Philly steak fries

Shaved ribeye, grilled onion and pepper, white American, served over fries with a side of horsey sauce.

Korean BBQ fries

Bulgogi beef, kimchi slaw, sesame, gochujang aioli, scallion. Heat is medium, not punishing.

Veggie chili fries

House black bean chili, sharp cheddar, sour cream, jalapeno, red onion. Vegetarian, can go vegan on request.

A short note

From Savannah.

Savannah ran kitchens around Columbus for years before buying the truck and painting it pink in her driveway. She still works the window most services, writes the specials on the chalkboard herself, and won't send a basket out that she wouldn't eat standing up.

— Savannah

Book Freaky Fries.

We started Freaky Fries because every food truck in Columbus seemed to chase the same tacos and the same burgers, and nobody was going hard on fries. We keep the menu short, the line moving, and the truck where we said it would be. If you booked us for a private event, the truck shows up early and leaves clean.
