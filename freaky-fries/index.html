What happens when you call.

The list

What we make.

Brisket cheese fries

Hand-cut russets, smoked brisket, cheddar sauce, pickled red onion, scallion. Served in a paper boat with a fork.

Nashville hot chicken fries

Buttermilk-fried chicken on top of crinkle cuts, hot oil glaze, dill pickle, ranch on the side.

Garlic parm basket

Double-fried shoestrings tossed in roasted garlic butter, parm, parsley, cracked pepper. Lemon wedge on the rim.

Birria loaded fries

Slow-braised beef, oaxaca cheese, cilantro, onion, with a side cup of consomé for dipping.

Sweet potato sundae

Sweet potato fries, cinnamon sugar, vanilla soft-serve, candied pecans, bourbon caramel drizzle. Eat it fast.

A short note

From Savannah.

Savannah ran fry station at two Columbus kitchens before buying the truck off a retiring owner in Macon and rewiring the whole flat-top herself. She does the prep, the window, and the books. If the truck is open, she is in it.

— Savannah

Book Freaky Fries.

We started Freaky Fries because Columbus had plenty of burger trucks and nobody doing fries the way they should be done, hand-cut, double-fried, loaded heavy. We park where the people are: Uptown at lunch, the Riverwalk after dark, Fort Moore on payday Fridays. One truck, one menu, cooked the same way every time.
