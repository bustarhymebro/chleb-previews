The list

What we make.

Loaded chili-cheese fries

Hand-cut fries, house chili, sharp cheddar sauce, diced onion, and a line of sour cream across the top.

Garlic parm basket

Fresh fries tossed in garlic butter, shaved parmesan, and chopped parsley. Side of ranch or marinara on request.

Buffalo chicken fries

Crispy chicken, buffalo sauce, blue cheese crumbles, and scallions piled over a full basket of seasoned fries.

Philly steak fries

Chopped ribeye, grilled onions and peppers, melted provolone, finished with a drizzle of garlic aioli.

Sweet potato sundae

Sweet potato fries, cinnamon sugar, marshmallow drizzle, and toasted pecans. The dessert basket people keep asking for.

A short note

From Savannah.

Savannah started Freaky Fries because every truck in Columbus was doing the same three things and nobody was treating fries like the main event. She cuts the potatoes herself, runs the window most shifts, and answers the phone when you call to book.

— Savannah

Book Freaky Fries.

We started Freaky Fries because Columbus has plenty of trucks doing tacos and barbecue and nobody putting in the work on a proper basket of fries. Every potato gets cut the morning of service, every sauce is made in-house, and the menu stays small so the line moves. If you have seen us at a lunch stop or a Saturday event, you already know the drill.
