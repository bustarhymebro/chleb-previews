About

How we run the truck in New Port Richey

We started Taco Movil to serve the New Port Richey crowd the kind of tacos we grew up eating, not the version built for a drive-thru. Every food truck in town gets judged on three things, is the meat right, is the salsa real, and did you answer the phone when someone wanted to book you. We try to get all three right, every shift. If you catch us at a lot or want us at your event, give us a call and we will tell you straight if we can do the date.
