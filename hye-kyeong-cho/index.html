About

Why Lacey families trust this piano studio

We started Hannah Cho Piano Studio because Lacey families kept ending up with instructors who taught from a workbook and nothing else. Most piano lessons around here run on canned method books and a teacher who has never actually performed. We build lessons around real technique and real repertoire, the same standard used on competition and concert stages, and we still make time to actually listen to how a student plays.

Meet Hye Kyeong

Hannah trained in South Korea and Bulgaria before landing in Lacey, and now teaches piano out of her own studio while serving as adjunct faculty at Saint Martin's University. She still performs and adjudicates competitions herself, so lessons come from someone who plays for a living, not just teaches theory.
