How Mr Tacos cooks in Pensacola

What happens when you call.

Order at the window
Tell us how many tacos, what protein, and if you want queso on the side.
Grilled to order
Steak hits the flat top, tortillas warm next to it, salsas and lime come out fresh.
Hot, in your hands
Plates and to-go trays handed over within minutes, never sitting under a heat lamp.

The list

What we make.

Steak tacos

Three grilled steak tacos on soft corn tortillas, onion, cilantro, salsa verde, lime wedge on the side.

Burro Burrito

Large flour tortilla, grilled steak with a crisp outside, rice, beans, queso, wrapped tight and griddled.

Loaded nachos

Fresh chips, queso dip, grilled steak, beans, pico, jalapenos on request. Sized for one big appetite or two normal ones.

Queso dip

House queso served warm with a basket of chips. Add as a side to any plate or take a pint to go.

Grilled steak plate

Steak grilled to order with rice, beans, and warm tortillas on the side. Build your own tacos at the table.
