How we run a Knoxville route

  • Weekly mow + edge
    Front and back mowed, beds and walks edged clean, trimmed around the AC unit, blown off the porch and drive.
  • Bi-weekly mow + edge
    Same package on a 14 day cycle. Works for shaded lots and yards that slow down in July and August.
  • Overgrown reset cut
    Knee-high yards stepped down in passes so the mower doesn't bog, then finished at a normal height the same day.
  • Vacant lot mow
    For rentals between tenants or listings going on market. We send a photo when the job's done so you don't have to drive over.
  • Spring + fall cleanup
    Sticks cleared, leaves cleaned out of bed lines, first or final cut of the season at the right height for dormancy.
