What happens when you call.

The list

What we make.

Smash burger

Two thin patties, American cheese, grilled onion, house sauce on a buttered potato bun. Comes with crinkle fries.

Pulled pork sandwich

Hand-pulled shoulder smoked overnight, sweet vinegar slaw, pickles, soft brioche. Served with a side of chips.

Loaded street fries

Crinkle-cut fries topped with cheese sauce, brisket bits, pickled jalapeno, and scallion. Single size, meant to share.

Chicken tender basket

Three hand-battered tenders, fries, and a choice of honey mustard, ranch, or hot sauce. House special on Fridays.

Veggie wrap

Grilled peppers, onion, black bean spread, pepper jack, and crema in a charred flour tortilla. The non-meat option that does not feel like one.

A short note

The owner runs the window, the grill, and the schedule, which is why the menu is short on purpose. We post next week's stops on Sunday night, pick up our own phone for bookings, and never run a route we cannot finish hot.

— Owner
