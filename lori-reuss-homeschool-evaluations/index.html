About

Why North Hills families call Lori

We started this because every homeschool family in the Pittsburgh area needs the same thing each spring, a real evaluator who knows the state requirements cold and won't make a kid nervous about it. Lori runs evaluations out of her home near Ross Park Mall, and keeps a running tutoring practice in reading, English, and math for students who need extra help between evaluations. Families come back year after year because the process is calm, thorough, and never feels like a test the kid can fail.

Meet Lori

Lori has evaluated home school students in all grades for the past five years, and spent seven years before that grading at Christian schools. She's a PA certified teacher who still tutors Title I students in reading and math when she's not doing evaluations.
