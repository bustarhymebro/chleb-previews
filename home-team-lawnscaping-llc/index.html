How we run yards in Buffalo

  • Weekly mow and edge
    Mow at proper height for the season, string-trim fence lines, edge walks and driveway, blow hardscapes clean. Same day each week.
  • Spring landscape contracting
    Bed redesigns, mulch refresh, fresh edging, shrub install, sod patching. Scoped on site, quoted flat, scheduled before May rush.
  • Fall cleanup package
    Full leaf removal, bed cutbacks, gutter clearing on request, final mow and edge. One flat price, hauled off the property.
  • Driveway and patio wash
    Surface-cleaner pass on concrete, spot treatment on oil and rust, rinse to the street. Most driveways finish in a single visit.
  • House and deck soft wash
    Low-pressure soft wash on vinyl, aluminum, and softwood decks. Pulls algae and pollen without forcing water behind the siding.
