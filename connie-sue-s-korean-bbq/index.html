The list

What we make.

Bulgogi box meal

Marinated ribeye off the flat-top, white or fried rice, two banchan, sesame and scallion finish.

Spicy pork box

Gochujang pork belly cooked to order, kimchi, pickled radish, choice of rice. Heat level called at the window.

Chicken bulgogi box

Soy garlic chicken thigh, rice, cucumber salad and sweet potato side. The box most first-timers order twice.

Kimbap roll

Hand-rolled that morning. Rice, egg, spinach, pickled radish, beef or tuna. Cut to order, no day-old rolls.

Korean corn dog

Mozzarella and sausage half-and-half, sugar-dusted, ketchup and mustard at the window. Made when you order it.

A short note

From Connie.

Connie grew up on her mother's cooking and started the truck to bring those dishes to Lakeland the way they actually taste at home in Korea. One of the first Korean food trucks in the area, still answering her own messages and still running the window most services.

— Connie

Book Connie Sue's Korean BBQ.

We started Connie Sue's because Lakeland did not have a Korean truck doing the dishes the way they are actually cooked at home. Every box gets built to order off the flat-top, the banchan is made in-house, and the rotation of stops is set a week at a time so regulars can plan around us. If you have not had Korean BBQ from a truck before, the window is the place to ask questions.
