The list

What we make.

Bulgogi rice bowl

Marinated ribeye over jasmine rice with kimchi, pickled radish, sesame slaw, fried egg, and a drizzle of gochujang aioli.

Spicy pork bibimbap

Gochujang-marinated pork, sauteed spinach, carrots, bean sprouts, fried egg, and crispy rice in the bottom of the bowl.

Korean BBQ tacos

Three flour tortillas with bulgogi or spicy pork, kimchi slaw, scallions, sesame seeds, and a side of sriracha mayo.

Kimchi loaded fries

Crispy fries topped with bulgogi, melted cheese, kimchi, scallions, gochujang aioli, and a sprinkle of toasted sesame.

Mandu plate

Six pan-fried pork and vegetable dumplings with soy-vinegar dipping sauce and a small side of house kimchi.

A short note

From Connie.

Connie grew up cooking with her mother and grandmother, and started the truck after years of friends asking her to cater. She marinates the bulgogi herself the night before every service, makes the kimchi in-house, and answers the phone when you call to book.
