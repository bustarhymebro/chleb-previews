The list

What we make.

Pad Thai

Rice noodles, egg, bean sprout, crushed peanut, lime. Pick your protein, pick your spice from 1 to 5. Around $12.

Kee Mao with beef

Wide rice noodles, basil, bell pepper, onion, beef in a dark drunken-noodle sauce. Built hot, dialed to your number.

Crab rangoon

Cream cheese and crab folded into wonton, fried to order while you wait. Comes with sweet chili on the side.

Thai tea

Brewed strong, sweetened with condensed milk, poured over ice. The thing you order to put out the fire from a level four.

Lunch combo plate

One noodle, one side of rangoon, a Thai tea. The standard order most regulars land on. Most plates run $10 to $20.
