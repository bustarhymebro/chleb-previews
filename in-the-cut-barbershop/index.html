The menu

How the chair runs in Dunedin

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Skin fade + beard line-up
Bald fade on the sides, scissor work on top, beard shaped and edged with a straight razor. About 45 minutes in the chair.
Classic taper + part
Low or mid taper, hard part cut in with the trimmer if you want one, side-swept finish with a matte clay.
Buzz + hot towel shave
One guard all over, beard taken down to the skin with a straight razor, hot and cold towels, balm. In and out in 30.
Kids cut (under 12)
Whatever style mom brought a picture of. Patient with first timers, no clipper-over-comb if the kid is squirming.
Gray blending
Soft blend through the temples and beard for guys who want to keep some salt without looking patchy. Lasts about three weeks.
