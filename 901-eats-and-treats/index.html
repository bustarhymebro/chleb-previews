How we run the truck in Memphis

What happens when you call.

Call the truck
Tell us the date, headcount, address, and whether you want plates or a buffet line.
Lock the menu
Pick three to five items, we send a quote and hold the date with a deposit.
We pull up
Truck arrives an hour early, sets up power and propane, opens the window on time.

The list

What we make.

Smoked rib tips basket

Hickory smoked tips, house barbecue sauce, white bread, pickles, and a side of slaw or fries.

Hot wing loaded fries

Crinkle fries under chopped wings, ranch drizzle, green onion, and a shake of lemon pepper on top.

Catfish plate

Two cornmeal dredged fillets, fried to order, with spaghetti or fries and two slices of white bread.

Turkey leg dinner

Smoked whole turkey leg with red beans and rice or mac, cornbread on the side, served in a foil boat.

Banana pudding cup

Vanilla wafers, fresh banana, made-that-morning pudding, whipped topping, packed in a twelve ounce cup with a lid.
