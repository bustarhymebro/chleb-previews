The list

What we make.

Smoked wings basket

Half pound of wood-smoked wings, house dry rub or sauce-tossed, served with pickles and a side of fries.

Brisket loaded fries

Hand-cut fries under chopped brisket, melted cheese, slaw, and a drizzle of house barbecue sauce. Fork required.

Hot honey chicken sandwich

Buttermilk-brined fried thigh, hot honey glaze, pickles, slaw, brioche bun. Comes with fries or a side.

Catfish plate

Two cornmeal-dusted catfish fillets fried to order, served with hush puppies, slaw, and white bread.

Banana pudding cup

Layered vanilla pudding, nilla wafers, fresh banana, fresh whipped cream. Made fresh the morning of each shift.

A short note

From Owner.

Owner has been cooking around Memphis kitchens for years before putting a window on a truck. The menu stays short on purpose, everything gets made the morning of, and the truck only posts up where the line moves and the food still comes out hot.

— Owner

Book 901 Eats and Treats.
