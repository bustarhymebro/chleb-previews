The list

What we make.

Carne asada tacos

Three tacos on doubled corn tortillas, marinated skirt steak off the flat top, onion, cilantro, lime, choice of salsa.

Pollo quesadilla

Flour tortilla, melted Oaxaca and Monterey jack, marinated chicken, served with green salsa and crema on the side.

Birria plate

Slow braised beef, three corn tortillas, cup of consomé for dipping, chopped onion and cilantro, lime wedge.

Loaded street fries

Fresh-cut fries, asada or pollo, melted cheese, pico, crema, jalapeños, choice of red or green salsa on top.

Salsa flight

Four house salsas with warm chips. Roasted tomato, chipotle, tomatillo verde, and a habanero-pineapple for the brave.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner started Salsa My Love after years of cooking salsas at home for friends who kept asking where they could buy a jar. Everything on the truck is made from scratch the morning of, and the salsas get bottled fresh each week.

— Owner
