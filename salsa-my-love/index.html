The list

What we make.

Carne asada street tacos

Three corn tortillas, marinated steak off the flat top, white onion, cilantro, salsa verde, lime on the side.

Pollo asado quesadilla

Flour tortilla pressed with chihuahua cheese and grilled chicken, served with a two ounce cup of roasted red salsa.

Birria ramen bowl

Slow braised beef over instant ramen in consomme, topped with onion, cilantro, and a wedge of lime.

Salsa flight

Four two ounce cups: roasted tomato, tomatillo verde, habanero pineapple, and the house red. Comes with chips.

Family taco pack

Twelve street tacos mixed asada and pollo, a pint of salsa, chips, and rice and beans for the table.

A short note

From Owner.

Salsa My Love is family run out of Largo. The owner started jarring salsas for friends out of a home kitchen, then put a truck on the road once the orders outgrew the counter. They answer the phone themselves and won't book a date they cannot personally cook.

— Owner

Book Salsa My Love.
