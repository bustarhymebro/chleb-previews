Las Virellas Food Truck

Run by Owner · Milwaukee

Las Virellas serves churrasco, mofongo, and jibaritos out of Zócalo Food Truck Park, feeding Milwaukee the way our abuelas cooked at home.

The list

What we make.

Churrasco

Skirt steak grilled over flame, sliced thin, served with chimichurri, rice, and a side of tostones or sweet plantains.

Mofongo

Green plantains mashed with garlic and pork crackling, packed into a dome, topped with stewed chicken or shrimp criollo.

Arroz con gandules

Yellow rice cooked down with pigeon peas, sofrito, and pork. The plate we make most. Comes as a side or a base.

Jibaritos sliders

Fried green plantains in place of bread, stacked with steak, garlic mayo, lettuce, and tomato. Sold as a pair.

Boricua Box

Sampler box built for one. Churrasco or chicken over rice and gandules, tostones, and a scoop of mofongo.

Tostones

Twice-fried green plantains, salted hot, served with garlic dipping sauce. The side everyone orders a second of.

A short note

From Owner.

We started Las Virellas in April 2022 because Milwaukee was missing the Puerto Rican plates we grew up eating. Four years later you can find the truck at Zócalo in Walker's Point most weekends, with the rice and gandules going from the time we open until we sell out.

— Owner
