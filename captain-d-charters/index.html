Local water, local guide

Donnie has been running boats on the Lake Okeechobee headwaters since well before most charter ops figured out where the bite actually was. He knows where the Stick Marsh hides crappie in the spring, where Garcia turns on for bass after a cold front, and where the bluegill stack up when the lake comes alive in May.

Trips run small. Most days it is you, your group, and the captain. No party-boat noise, no shared deck space, no schedule pressure to clear off so the next charter can launch. The boat goes where the fish are that morning, even if that means a longer ride to a spot the other guides skipped.

Pontoon tours work the same way. Donnie picks the route based on the wind, the time of year, and what your group wants to see. Sunset runs out of Okeechobee. Quiet morning loops through the marsh. Family days with rods on board for the kids. The lake is bigger than most people realize once you get past the launch ramps.
