About

How the truck runs in Clearwater

We started Annie Get Your Bun because every truck on the Pinellas circuit was running the same frozen brioche from the same supplier. We bake our own buns the morning of every shift, smoke our own meat, and keep the menu small enough that nothing sits. If you've eaten with us once, the second visit tastes the same.

Meet Annie

Annie started baking buns out of her kitchen for friends, then bought the truck after the third weekend in a row of running out by noon. She bakes every bun the morning of service and won't run a shift with day-old bread.
