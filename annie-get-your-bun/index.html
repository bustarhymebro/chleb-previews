The list

What we make.

Hot dogs

All-beef dog on a steamed bun, ketchup mustard relish onions, served from the propane grill in a paper sleeve.

Burgers

Quarter-pound patty, American cheese, the usual fixings on request. Cooked when you order it, not before.

Sausage & peppers

Sweet Italian sausage, sauteed onions and peppers, crusty roll. The order Annie's regulars phone in across the market.

Popcorn & pretzels

Hot popcorn by the bag and soft pretzels with yellow mustard, ready while you wait for the grill.

Cold drinks

Cans on ice in the cooler under the tent. Water, soda, whatever pairs with a dog in the Florida sun.

A short note

From Annie.

Annie has been in the food business for 37 years and has been running this propane hot dog cart around Clearwater and St. Pete since the early 2010s. It's a mom and pop operation, a cart not a truck, so she can set up just about anywhere a tent will fit.

— Annie
