A short note

From Owner.

Owner started this truck because Charlottesville deserved a real street taco, not a chain version with pre-made salsa from a bag. Every batch of al pastor is marinated in-house overnight, and the tortillas are pressed fresh each morning before the window opens. If the guac runs out, we close the window until a new batch is ready.

— Owner

We started rolling through Charlottesville because the taco options here were either fast food or a forty-minute wait at a sit-down spot. Our truck is on the street six days a week, the menu is short on purpose, and everything is made to order at the window.
