About

Why Oregon City homeschool families call Rachel

We started this because too many Oregon City homeschool families were stuck calling around every spring, leaving voicemails with test examiners who never called back. Testing your kids for state compliance should not feel like a scavenger hunt. We keep a simple calendar, answer our own phone, and get your results back fast enough that a deadline never sneaks up on you.

Meet Rachel

Rachel got into homeschool testing after helping her own kids' co-op find someone reliable to administer the yearly exam. She keeps appointments short, explains scores in plain English, and never makes a family feel like they're being graded too.
