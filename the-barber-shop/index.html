How Bartlett has gotten haircuts since 1967

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Men's haircut
Wash optional, clipper or scissor work, neck shaved with a straight razor, finished with powder and a brush down.
Hot towel shave
Pre-shave oil, two hot towels, lathered with a brush, straight-razor pass with the grain and against, balm to close.
Cut and shave combo
Full haircut paired with a hot shave in the same seat. Roughly forty-five minutes start to finish. Walk-in friendly.
Eyebrow clean-up
Trimmed and tidied with shears, added on at the end of a cut or shave. Three minutes in the chair, no extra fuss.
Father and son walk-in
Two chairs going at once when both are open, dad and kid in and out together. Same price as two regulars.
