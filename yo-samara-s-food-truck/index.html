Yo Samara's Food Truck

Run by Samara · Fort Myers

Yo Samara's rolls into Fort Myers lots, breweries, and backyards with a from-scratch Mediterranean menu built around one hot oven and a short prep list.

How we feed Fort Myers off the truck

What happens when you call.

Call or text
Tell us the date, headcount, and the lot or address. We hold the slot same day.
Lock the menu
Pick a two or three item menu, confirm dietary swaps, and put down a deposit.
We roll up
Truck arrives an hour early, sets up power and water, serves hot off the line.

The list

What we make.

Chicken shawarma pita

House-marinated thigh off the vertical spit, garlic toum, pickled turnip, shredded lettuce, soft pita warmed on the flat top.

Lamb gyro plate

Shaved lamb and beef gyro over saffron rice, cucumber salad, hummus, tzatziki, charred pita on the side.

Falafel wrap

Fried-to-order chickpea falafel, tahini, tomato, parsley, quick-pickled onion, wrapped in a fresh pita. Vegan as served.

Loaded hummus bowl

Warm hummus, your choice of chicken or falafel, olive oil, sumac, parsley, paprika, served with two warm pitas.

Baklava + Turkish coffee

House-rolled pistachio baklava cut in small squares. Pulled hot Turkish coffee or iced mint tea on the side.

A short note

From Samara.

Samara has been cooking this menu for years before the truck, first out of her kitchen for friends and then for catering trays around Fort Myers. She runs the line herself, answers the booking phone, and won't serve anything she didn't prep that morning.

— Samara

Book Yo Samara's Food Truck.

We started Yo Samara's because every food truck in Fort Myers seemed to be running the same three menus, and nobody was cooking the Mediterranean food we grew up on. The truck runs a tight menu on purpose so every plate comes out hot, fast, and the way it should. If you've eaten with us at a brewery once, you already know the rhythm.
