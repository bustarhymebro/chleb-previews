What happens when you call.

The list

What we make.

Signature plate

Protein over rice, house slaw, pickled onions, and a side of plantains. The plate the regulars order without looking at the board.

Loaded fries

Hand-cut fries, chopped protein, melted cheese, crema, and a scatter of cilantro. Built for the walk back to the car.

Street tacos

Three soft corn tortillas, choice of protein, white onion, cilantro, lime. Salsa verde or roja on the side.

Rice bowl

Cilantro lime rice, beans, protein, slaw, and salsa. The lunch order for anyone eating at their desk.

Family pack

Feeds four, your pick of two proteins, rice, beans, tortillas, and salsas. Ready in twenty minutes if you call ahead.
