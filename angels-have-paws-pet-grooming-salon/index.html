What we're known for

Specialties

Full-service dog grooming
Bath, blow-dry, breed-standard or custom cut, nail trim, ear cleaning, and a sanitary trim included.
Cat grooming
Bath, lion cut or trim on request, nail caps available, de-shedding for the heavy shedders, handled without sedation.
Rabbit grooming
Nail trim, brush-out, scent gland check, and a light bath for rabbits that tolerate water, done slow.
Grooming for difficult or challenging pets
Extra time built into the appointment, muzzle-free where possible, breaks as needed, staff trained to read stress before it escalates.
Grooming for dogs with medical issues
We work around vet restrictions, skin conditions, arthritis, and post-surgery limits, shorter sessions when the dog needs it.
Bird grooming
Nail and beak trim, wing trim on request, handled by staff comfortable with feathers, not just fur.

About

Why Tallahassee trusts the hard cases here

We started Angels Have Paws because too many groomers in Tallahassee rush the appointment, especially with pets who are scared, aggressive, or just old and slow to move. We built the shop around the animals that get turned away elsewhere, the ones who need a quieter room and a groomer who won't force it. A few hundred regulars trust us with the pets other places won't touch, and we plan to keep it that way.

Meet Lori

Lori is a Nationally Certified Master Groomer running an AKC S.A.F.E. certified shop, and she built it around the pets other salons turn down. She and her staff take the slow route with the nervous, the elderly, and the medically fragile ones.
