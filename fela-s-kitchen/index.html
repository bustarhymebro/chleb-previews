Fela's Kitchen runs a one-truck operation out of Clearwater, cooking jollof, suya, and plantain to order for lunch crowds and weekend events.

How the truck runs in Clearwater

What happens when you call.

Check the post
We post tomorrow's location and menu the night before on the phone line and socials.
Order at the window
Walk up, order, pay. Most plates come out in six to eight minutes.
Eat hot
Grab a seat nearby or take it back to the office. Containers travel well.

The list

What we make.

Jollof rice + chicken

Smoky party-style jollof, grilled chicken thigh, fried plantain, and a side of pepper sauce on request.

Suya beef skewers

Thin-sliced beef rubbed in yaji peanut spice, grilled over open flame, served with sliced onion and tomato.

Egusi + pounded yam

Melon seed stew with spinach and goat meat, paired with a fresh ball of pounded yam. Friday only.

Puff puff (half dozen)

Hand-rolled fried dough, lightly sweet, dusted with powdered sugar. Made in batches through the lunch rush.

Plantain + black-eyed peas

Sweet ripe plantain with stewed beans in palm oil and pepper. Vegetarian plate, full portion size.

A short note

From Fela.

Fela cooked at home for friends in Clearwater for years before putting the truck on the road. She runs the window herself most days, answers her own phone, and won't send out a plate of jollof she wouldn't serve her own family.

— Fela

Book Fela's Kitchen.

We started Fela's Kitchen because Clearwater had no good spot for proper West African food, and we got tired of driving to Tampa for jollof. The truck moves around Pinellas through the week, but the cooking stays the same. One pot at a time, made the way Fela's mother taught her.
