About

Why Paramus families choose real training

We started this studio because too many Paramus families told us the same story, a piano teacher who changed every semester or taught straight from a workbook nobody cared about. Jacqueline trained at Juilliard and Mannes College of Music and still performs professionally, so every lesson here comes from someone who plays for a living, not just teaches from a book. We keep the same teacher, the same weekly slot, and an actual plan for where the student is headed, whether that means a school recital or finally finishing a piece they always wanted to play.

Meet Jacqueline

Jacqueline trained at Juilliard under Peter Serkin and graduated from Mannes College of Music before founding her Paramus studio. She still performs with the Alacorde Piano Trio and brings that same stage discipline to every lesson, whether the student is six or sixty.
