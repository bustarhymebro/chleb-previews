The list

What we make.

Chimi De Beef (Res)

Seasoned beef, cabbage, tomato, onion, house pink sauce, pressed on a soft pan de agua bun. $18.99.

Tambora Yaroa

Layered yuca fries with cheese, ketchup, mayo, and your choice of meat, the late-night Santiago classic. $17.99.

El Matatan Patacon

A green plantain sandwich smashed thin and crisp, stacked with meat and toppings. Eats like a meal. $20.99.

Mofongo Bowl de Res

Garlic plantain mash topped with stewed beef, plated in a bowl so every bite gets sauce. $24.99.

Picalonga Pa Comensar

Starter sharing platter of longaniza, carnita, chicharron, and tostones for the table. $28.99.

Empanadas De Beef or Chicken

Hand-folded empanadas fried to order, beef or chicken with cheese. $4.50 each, easy add-on.
