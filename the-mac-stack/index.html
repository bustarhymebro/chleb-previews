The list

What we make.

The Classic Stack

Three-cheese mac base, toasted breadcrumb top, served in a bowl. The one we judge a new lot by.

Brisket Mac

Chopped smoked brisket layered over the classic base, finished with a spoon of pan jus and pickled onion.

Buffalo Chicken Mac

Hand-pulled fried chicken, hot sauce toss, blue cheese crumble, celery on top. Heat is real but not a stunt.

Pulled Pork Mac

Slow-pulled pork shoulder, house barbecue sauce, crispy onion. Comes with a side of pickles to cut the richness.

Loaded Bacon Mac

Thick-cut bacon, sharp cheddar, green onion, sour cream drizzle. The one most kids order without asking what's in it.
