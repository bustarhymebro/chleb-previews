The menu

How we run the chair in Indianapolis

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Men's Haircut
Full consultation, shampoo if requested, scissor or clipper cut, neckline cleaned, hot towel finish at the chair.
Fades
Skin, bald, low, mid, or high fade. Blended by hand with multiple guards, no clipper-over-comb shortcuts.
Beard Trim
Shape-up along the cheek line and neck, scissor work on the length, beard oil worked in before you stand up.
Hot Towel Shave
Steamed towel, pre-shave oil, lather, straight razor with the grain and against, cold towel and balm to close.
Line-ups
Edge work on the hairline, temples, and beard. Straight razor finish so the line stays sharp through the week.
