What we're known for

Specialties

Jerk chicken plate
Quarter chicken marinated overnight, charred on the flat-top, served over rice and peas with cabbage and plantain.
Oxtail and rice
Braised four hours in brown gravy with butter beans, scooped over rice and peas with steamed cabbage on the side.
Curry goat plate
Bone-in goat slow-cooked in yellow curry and scotch bonnet, plated with rice and peas and fried plantain.
Brown stew snapper
Whole snapper fried then simmered in brown stew gravy with peppers and onions, served over white rice.
Jerk wings basket
Six jerk wings, festival dumpling on the side, mango habanero or jerk aioli for dipping. Walk-up window order.

About

How we run the truck in Fayetteville

We started Island Vibes because Fayetteville had no shortage of good food trucks but nowhere to get a real jerk plate without a two-hour drive. We cook every plate to order off the truck, post the route at the start of each week, and pick up the phone when you call about catering. If we are out of oxtail by 7 PM on a Saturday, that is the day we had.

Meet Kemar

Kemar grew up cooking jerk over pimento wood at home in Jamaica, moved to Northwest Arkansas, and got tired of driving to Tulsa for a real plate. He runs the truck himself, takes the calls himself, and won't send food out of the window he wouldn't eat standing next to it.
