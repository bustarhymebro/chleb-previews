How we run yards in Erie

  • Weekly mow + edge package
    Mow, line trim, hard-edge the concrete, blow off walks and drive. Same crew, same day of the week, all season.
  • Bi-weekly maintenance plan
    Every other week visit for smaller lots or shaded yards. Mow, trim, blow. Skipped weeks rolled into a cleanup.
  • Spring cleanup
    Leaf and stick removal, bed clearing, dethatch pass on request, first mow of the year cut a little long.
  • Fall leaf haul
    Two-pass leaf removal, gutters cleared from the ground, mulch-mow on smaller leaf loads. Bagged and hauled, no piles left.
  • Mulch refresh
    Hardwood or dyed mulch by the yard, beds re-edged, two-inch fresh layer. Priced by yard delivered and installed.
