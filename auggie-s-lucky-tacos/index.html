Auggie's Lucky Tacos

Run by Owner · Marietta

Auggie's Lucky Tacos runs a tight food truck out of Marietta, slinging real-deal street tacos for lunch rushes, office parks, and private parties.

The list

What we make.

Al pastor taco

Marinated pork off the trompo, double corn tortilla, white onion, cilantro, charred pineapple, salsa verde on top.

Carne asada plate

Grilled skirt steak, three tortillas, rice, charro beans, grilled jalapeno and onion, lime wedge, salsa roja.

Birria quesatacos

Slow-braised beef, melted Oaxaca cheese, crispy-fried tortilla, side of consomme for dipping. Three per order.

Pollo asado burrito

Citrus-marinated chicken, rice, beans, cheese, pico, crema. Wrapped, pressed on the flattop until the outside crisps.

Taco bar catering

Two proteins, rice, beans, four salsas, chips, all the fixings. Priced per head, twenty-five guest minimum.
