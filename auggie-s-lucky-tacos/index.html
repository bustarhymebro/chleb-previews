How we run the truck in Marietta

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Parked at 4852 Canton Rd, Marietta, Wednesday through Saturday. Check the page before you drive.
Order at the window
Pick your meat, pick your platform: taco, quesadilla, fries, or nachos. We call your name.
Eat it hot
Grab a salsa cup, find a spot, hit it while the tortilla is still warm off the press.

The list

What we make.

Al Pastor Tacos

Marinated pork shaved off the trompo style, onions, cilantro, lime. Two per order on warm corn tortillas.

Carne Asada Fries

Fresh-cut fries under chopped asada, melted cheese, salsa, and a squeeze of lime. Plate-sized, fork required.

Campechana

Mixed-meat taco built for folks who can't pick one. Asada and chorizo or al pastor, doubled up.

Naked Nachos

Chips, seasoned meat, cheese, salsa. No filler, no sour cream firehose. The seasoning is the move here.

Homemade Salsas

Red and green made in-house every service day. Bring heat tolerance, ask before you dump the whole cup on.

A short note

From Owner.

Auggie runs the truck himself out of 4852 Canton Road. He keeps the menu short on purpose, presses tortillas to order, and makes the red and green salsas the morning of service. If the line is long, it's because he refuses to rush a taco.

— Owner
