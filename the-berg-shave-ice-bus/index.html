The list

What we make.

Tiger's Blood

Strawberry, watermelon, and coconut layered over fresh-shaved snow. The classic shave ice flavor and our top seller.

Wedding Cake

Almond and vanilla syrup with a coconut cream snowcap. Tastes like a slice of frosted cake in a cup.

Blue Hawaii

Pineapple and blue raspberry over snow-soft ice. Add a coconut cream drizzle for the full island version.

Cherry Limeade

Tart lime and black cherry, finished with a squeeze of fresh lime. The summer-on-a-baseball-field cup.

Sno-Cap upgrade

Sweetened condensed milk poured over any flavor. Adds a creamy top layer that soaks down through the ice.

A short note

From Owner.

The Owner runs the bus solo most weekends, shaving every cup to order from a block of ice instead of a machine hopper. The bus is a retired short school bus, rebuilt with a serving window, a real shave head, and a syrup rail that holds the full flavor list.

— Owner

Book The 'Berg Shave Ice Bus.

We started The 'Berg because Wichita summers are long and hot and there was nowhere near home to get real Hawaiian-style shave ice, the kind shaved fresh off a block instead of crunched out of a machine. The bus shows up at events, ballfields, and parties across the metro all season. If you can find us a flat patch of pavement and an outlet or a little room for the generator, we can serve a line.
