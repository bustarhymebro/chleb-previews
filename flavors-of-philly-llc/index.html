The list

What we make.

Original cheesesteak

Ribeye, grilled onions, American or provolone, on a hoagie roll. Wit or witout, your call at the window.

Teriyaki cheesesteak

Same ribeye, finished in teriyaki glaze with onions and cheese. House signature, only place in Columbus running it.

Philly water ice

Real water ice, not snow cone, not Italian gelato. Hand-scooped, cup or bowl, finishes the meal right.

Cheesesteak combo

Cheesesteak plus chips, soda, and water ice for around $20. Most-ordered ticket at the window.

Chips and soda

Cold sodas and bagged chips to round out a sandwich. Grab-and-go pricing, no upcharge games.
