How we run the truck in Columbus

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Check the week's schedule, we post lunch and dinner stops every Sunday night.
Order at the window
Tell us wit or witout, pick your cheese, grab a number, takes about six minutes.
Eat it hot
Pick up at the side window, sauces and napkins on the truck, eat it before it cools.

The list

What we make.

Classic cheesesteak

Chopped ribeye, melted American or provolone, grilled onions, on a soft hoagie roll. Wit or witout, your call.

Chicken cheesesteak

Chopped chicken thigh, sharp provolone, sweet peppers, hoagie roll. Add long hots if you want it loud.

Italian hoagie

Capicola, salami, ham, provolone, lettuce, tomato, onion, oil and vinegar, oregano. Cold, sharp, no shortcuts.

Loaded fries basket

Hand-cut fries under chopped steak, melted cheese, grilled onions. Sized to share but you probably won't.

Roast pork Italian

Slow-roasted pork, sharp provolone, garlicky broccoli rabe on a seeded roll. Made when we get the rabe in.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner grew up on Philly corner-shop cheesesteaks and got tired of explaining to friends in Columbus what the real version tastes like. So the truck is the answer. Ribeye gets chopped to order, rolls come in fresh, and nothing sits under a heat lamp.

— Owner

Book Flavors of Philly LLC.

We started Flavors of Philly because Columbus has plenty of barbecue and plenty of burgers, but a real chopped-ribeye cheesesteak is harder to find than it should be. We chop the steak to order, toast the rolls, and keep the menu tight on purpose. If the line is long, it's because every sandwich is built one at a time.
