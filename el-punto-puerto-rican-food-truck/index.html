El Punto Puerto Rican Food Truck

El Punto rolls into Newberry and west Gainesville with mofongo, pernil, and the kind of plates your abuela would actually sign off on.

The list

What we make.

Mofongo with pernil

Green plantains mashed with garlic and chicharrón, topped with slow-roasted pork shoulder and a ladle of ajo sauce.

Pernil plate

Roasted pork shoulder over arroz con gandules with a side of sweet plantains and a cup of pickled onions.

Jibarito sandwich

Twice-fried green plantains pressed around steak, lettuce, tomato, and garlic mayo. Eat it standing up, over the wrapper.

Pollo guisado plate

Bone-in chicken stewed in sofrito, olives, and potatoes, served over white rice with red beans on the side.

Alcapurrias and bacalaitos

Fresh-fried green banana fritters stuffed with seasoned beef, plus salt cod fritters by the half dozen.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner has been cooking Puerto Rican food in north central Florida for years, and started El Punto after getting tired of driving to Orlando for a real plate of pernil. The truck is family run, the sofrito is made fresh every week, and the menu does not change to chase trends.

— Owner

Book El Punto Puerto Rican Food Truck.

We cook the way our families cook, with sofrito made from scratch and pork that sits in the oven for hours before the lunch window opens. If you have eaten on the island, you will recognize the food.
