The list

What we make.

Cubano + plantain plate

Pressed Cubano on Cuban bread, sweet maduros, side of black beans. Pickle, mustard, ham, mojo pork, swiss.

Mojo pork bowl

Slow-roasted citrus mojo pork over yellow rice and black beans, topped with pickled onion and cilantro.

Pollo a la plancha

Marinated chicken thigh seared on the flat top, rice, beans, and a side of garlic mojo.

Ropa vieja platter

Shredded beef stewed with peppers and onions in tomato sofrito, served with rice, beans, and maduros.

Empanada trio

Three hand-folded empanadas, choice of beef picadillo, chicken, or cheese. Comes with house cilantro sauce.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner has been cooking Latin plates around Jacksonville for years, started the truck after running kitchens where the mojo got watered down. Picks up the phone, preps the pork overnight, and refuses to serve rice that has been sitting more than an hour.

— Owner
