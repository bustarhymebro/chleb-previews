How we run yards across Lubbock

  • Weekly mow + edge package
    Full mow, blade edge on curbs and bed lines, blowdown of all hard surfaces. Billed per visit.
  • Monthly mulch refresh
    Pull weeds, top up mulch depth in landscape beds, and edge the border between turf and bed.
  • Spring yard cleanup
    Rake and remove winter debris, dethatch the turf surface, cut back dead ornamental growth before warm season starts.

    Pre-emergent or post-emergent granular application timed to the calendar and what the turf is showing at visit.

    Trim all foundation shrubs to their natural shape, haul the cuttings, and clean up the surrounding bed edge.
