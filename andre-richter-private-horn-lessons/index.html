What we're known for

Specialties

Embouchure fundamentals

All-District audition rep

Double horn technique

About

How lessons run at Andre's Raleigh studio

We started this studio because most horn students in Raleigh only get a few rushed minutes with the horn during a band class, and the horn does not forgive rushed instruction. Every lesson here is one-on-one, built around what that student actually needs next, whether that is a steady embouchure or a clean audition etude. We keep a manageable roster so nobody is squeezed into a rushed half hour.

Meet Andre

Andre came up through Wake County band and orchestra programs before teaching horn full time. He keeps his roster small and picks up his own phone for scheduling.
