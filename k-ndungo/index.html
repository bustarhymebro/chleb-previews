How the truck runs in Pasco

What happens when you call.

The list

What we make.

Mofongo bowl

Green plantains mashed with garlic and chicharrón, topped with your choice of stewed chicken, pork, or shrimp in criollo sauce. Also offered.

Pernil plate

Slow-roasted pork shoulder pulled to order, served with arroz con gandules and a side of sweet plantains. Also offered.

Tripleta sandwich

Three meats, lettuce, tomato, shoestring potatoes, pressed on a soft roll with our house mayo-ketchup. Also offered.

Catering package

Truck on site for two to four hours, two proteins, two sides, plantains and rice for your guest count. Headcount confirmed at booking.

Tostones and sides

Twice-fried green plantains with garlic mojo, plus alcapurrias and empanadillas pulled from the fryer all service. Also offered.

A short note

From Owner.

The chef behind K'ndungo grew up on this food in Puerto Rico and started the truck three years ago to cook it the way it's supposed to be cooked. Sofrito made in-house, pernil that sits in the oven for hours, no shortcuts in the fryer.

— Owner

Book K'ndungo.

We started K'ndungo because there was nowhere around Zephyrhills cooking the Puerto Rican food we actually eat at home. So we built the truck, wrote the menu around abuela's recipes, and started rotating between Epperson, Wesley Chapel, and the Tampa side. Three years in and we still cook every plate to order.
