Services

How the shop runs in Hamilton

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Haircut + styling
Consultation, wash optional, scissor or clipper cut, neck cleanup, styled and finished before you leave the chair.
Straight razor neck shave
Add-on to any haircut. Hot lather around the ears and hairline, finished with balm. Sharper line than a trimmer can hold.
Beard trim + line-up
Length set with shears, cheek and neck lines cut with the straight razor, mustache shaped to sit clean over the lip.
Traditional hot shave
Pre-shave oil, steam towel, hand-whipped lather, two-pass straight razor, cold towel and aftershave. About forty minutes in the chair.
Classic facial
Hot towel, cleanse, exfoliation, massage, finishing balm. Pairs with a hot shave or stands on its own.
