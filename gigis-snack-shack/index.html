The list

What we make.

Chili cheese fries

Crinkle fries loaded with beef chili, shredded cheddar, pickled jalapenos, and sour cream on the side.

Chicken tender basket

Three hand-breaded tenders fried to order, served with seasoned fries and your choice of dipping sauce.

Jumbo dog with the works

Half-pound beef frank on a toasted bun with mustard, relish, diced onions, and shredded cheddar.

Loaded street nachos

Tortilla chips layered with queso, seasoned ground beef, pico de gallo, and pickled jalapenos.

Pretzel bites and cheese dip

Soft-baked pretzel bites, salted and served warm with a side of house white cheddar dipping sauce.

A short note

From Jason.

Jason has been running Gigis Snack Shack since he decided a full sit-down restaurant was too much overhead and a drive-through was too much compromise. He cooks everything to order from the truck window, keeps the menu to what he does well, and moves the truck to where the regulars already know to find him around New Bern.

, Jason
