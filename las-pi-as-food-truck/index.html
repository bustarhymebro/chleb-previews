Las Piñas brings tacos, tortas, and aguas frescas to Tuscaloosa lots, game days, and private events with a small crew that cooks every order to order.

The list

What we make.

Al pastor taco plate

Three corn tortillas, marinated pork off the trompo, onion, cilantro, pineapple, salsa verde, and a lime wedge.

Birria quesatacos

Slow braised beef, melted Oaxaca on a griddled corn tortilla, side of consome for dipping. Three per order.

Carne asada torta

Grilled steak on a toasted telera with beans, avocado, queso fresco, pickled jalapeno, and chipotle crema.

Elote in a cup

Sweet corn off the cob, butter, mayo, cotija, chili lime seasoning, fresh lime. Served warm in a clear cup.

Agua fresca pitcher

Horchata, jamaica, or pineapple. Made fresh that morning, sold by the cup or the gallon for events.

A short note

From Owner.

Las Piñas is run by a small family crew that has been cooking Mexican food in Tuscaloosa for years before the truck. We marinate the pastor overnight, press tortillas the morning of service, and refuse to plate anything we would not serve our own table.

— Owner

Book Las Piñas Food Truck.

We started Las Piñas because Tuscaloosa had plenty of Tex-Mex sit-downs and almost nowhere to grab real street tacos on a lunch break. We run one truck, a tight menu, and we cook everything to order on a flat top you can see from the window. If we run out by two, we run out, and we are back the next day.
