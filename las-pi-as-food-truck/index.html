How the truck runs in Tuscaloosa

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Check the week's schedule for the lot we are parked in around Tuscaloosa or Northport.
Order at the window
Call out a plate or a tray. Cash and card both work, ten minute ticket times most days.
Eat hot
Plates come straight off the flattop. Grab a seat curbside or take it back to the office.

The list

What we make.

Pork BBQ skewers

Three marinated skewers grilled to order, served over garlic rice with a side of spiced vinegar.

Lumpia by the dozen

Hand rolled pork and vegetable lumpia, fried at the window, packed with sweet chili on the side.

Chicken adobo plate

Bone in chicken thighs braised in soy, vinegar, garlic, and bay, plated over rice with pickled cucumber.

Pancit bihon tray

Rice noodles tossed with cabbage, carrots, and pork, finished with calamansi. Half tray feeds eight, full feeds sixteen.

Sisig rice bowl

Crispy chopped pork with onion, chili, and a runny egg over garlic rice. Available Friday and Saturday only.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner runs the truck themselves, taking orders at the window and cooking on the line. Recipes come from family, not a commissary playbook. If the rice is out, the day is done, no shortcuts on the plate.

— Owner

Book Las Piñas Food Truck.

We started Las Piñas because Tuscaloosa did not have a Filipino kitchen you could walk up to and order from. Every other truck in town runs tacos or burgers, and we wanted to put lumpia and adobo on the same lunch map. The truck is small on purpose, the menu stays tight, and the food goes out the window the way it would come off a stove at home.
