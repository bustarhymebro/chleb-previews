How the truck runs in Tuscaloosa

What happens when you call.

Check the post
Weekly schedule drops Monday with the lots and hours for each day.
Roll up
Order at the window, cash or card, we call your name when the plate is up.
Eat hot
Picnic tables when the lot has them, otherwise the tailgate works fine.

The list

What we make.

Chicken adobo plate

Braised thigh in soy and vinegar, garlic rice, pickled veg on the side. Comes with two lumpia.

Pancit bihola

Rice noodles tossed with cabbage, carrot, and chicken. Lemon wedge, served in a folded paper boat.

Lumpia by the dozen

Hand rolled pork and vegetable spring rolls, fried to order. Sweet chili on the side, packed for travel.

Pork sisig bowl

Chopped pork shoulder griddled with onion and chili, over rice, finished with a soft egg and calamansi.

Halo halo cup

Shaved ice with sweet beans, jackfruit, leche flan, and ube. Built in a clear cup so you see the layers.

A short note

From Owner.

The owner started Las Piñas to bring the food they grew up eating to Tuscaloosa, the kind of plates you find at a family party in Manila. The truck runs lean, the recipes do not get shortcut, and the schedule goes out every Monday.

— Owner
