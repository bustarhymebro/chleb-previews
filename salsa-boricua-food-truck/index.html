The list

What we make.

Pernil plate

Slow roasted pork shoulder, arroz con gandules, sweet plantains, and a side of pique. Served with a slice of avocado in season.

Tripleta sandwich

Pork, ham, and steak on pan sobao with lettuce, tomato, queso, potato sticks, and the house mayo-ketchup. Pressed hot.

Mofongo with pollo guisado

Green plantains mashed with garlic, olive oil, and chicharrón, topped with stewed chicken in sofrito. Comes with a cup of broth.

Jibarito steak

Flattened plantain bread, thin sliced steak, lettuce, tomato, garlic aioli. Cut in half and wrapped for one-hand eating.

Alcapurrias and bacalaitos

Fried green banana fritters stuffed with seasoned beef, plus cod fritters by the bag. Sold by the piece or the half dozen.

A short note

From Richard.

Richard runs the truck himself, his recipes come from his mother's kitchen in Bayamón before the family settled in Florida. He still does the marinades the night before, picks up his own phone for catering, and won't serve a plate of rice he wouldn't eat himself.

— Richard

Book Salsa Boricua Food Truck.

We started Salsa Boricua because Belleview and the rest of Marion County didn't have a real Puerto Rican kitchen, just rumors of one two counties over. Every plate goes out the window the same way it would leave a family table at home, seasoned the night before and cooked to order. If we run out, we run out, that means it was fresh.
