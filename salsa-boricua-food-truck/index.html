Salsa Boricua serves real Boricua cooking from our truck and kitchen in Belleview, pernil, tripletas, and pollo guisado made the way Richard's family taught him.

Why Belleview keeps coming back to the truck

What happens when you call.

Find the truck
Hwy 484 most days, Eat Street and private events on the weekends. Check before you drive out.
Order at the window
Plates, sandwiches, and empanadas off the board, cash or card, ready in a few minutes.
Eat hot
Picnic tables on site, or take it back to the job and finish it in the truck.

The list

What we make.

Pernil (Roast Pork) plate

Slow roasted pork shoulder, yellow rice with pigeon peas, sweet plantains on the side. $5 plate.

Tripleta sandwich

Steak, ham, and roast pork on Cuban bread with queso, lettuce, tomato, and potato sticks, pressed hot. $7.

Bistec con Cebolla

Thin steak smothered in sauteed onions and pan gravy, served over yellow rice with pigeon peas. $5 plate.

Pollo Guisado

Chicken stewed down with sofrito, potatoes, olives, and tomato, ladled over rice. $5 plate.

Cuban sandwich

Roast pork, ham, swiss, pickles, and mustard on Cuban bread, pressed until the cheese pulls.

Empanadas and Italian ice

Beef and chicken empanadas fried to order, plus shaved Italian ice in a rotation of fruit flavors.

A short note

From Richard.

Richard runs Salsa Boricua as authentic a Puerto Rican food truck as they come, parked across from the McDonald's and the new Popeyes on Highway 484. Nine years in, he still cooks the pernil himself and answers the phone for catering orders.
