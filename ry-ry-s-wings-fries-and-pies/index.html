A short note

From Ryan.

I'm Ryan, and I built this truck around the three things I cooked at every cookout growing up in Birmingham: wings, fries, and pie. I kept the menu short on purpose, because I watched too many trucks try to please everyone and end up doing nothing right. I run every shift myself, cook every order to order, and the truck does not roll until the pies are done.

, Ryan

Book Ry Ry's Wings Fries and Pies.

We started Ry Ry's because Birmingham deserved a truck that took wings, fries, and pies seriously as a full menu, not a novelty. Most spots in town rush at least one of those three and it shows on the plate. We park across the city every week, cook everything to order, and keep the line moving without cutting corners on the food. If it is not right, it does not leave the window.
