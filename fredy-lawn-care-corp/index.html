The plan list

How we run lawns in Fort Myers

  • Weekly mow and edge package
    Mow, line trim around fences and posts, hard edge along driveway and walk, blow off all surfaces. Forty eight weeks a year.
  • Monthly mulch refresh
    Top dress beds with fresh mulch, pull visible weeds, recut bed lines. Keeps the front looking new between full refreshes.
  • Hedge shape and palm trim
    Ficus, clusia, and hibiscus shaped to spec. Sabal and queen palms cleaned of seed pods and dead fronds, debris hauled.
  • Full yard cleanup
    Overgrown grass cut down in stages, beds weeded, fence lines cleared, debris bagged and removed. Yard ready for a weekly route.
  • Sod patch and repair
    Cut out dead St. Augustine, level the soil, lay fresh sod, water in. Small patches up to half pallet handled same week.
