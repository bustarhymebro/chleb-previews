The list

What we make.

Chicken lime cilantro tacos

Grilled chicken, fresh lime, chopped cilantro, on warm tortillas. The taco the Little's Grill folks wrote home about.

BBQ pork sliders

Slow-cooked pork, house sauce, homemade coleslaw on top, served three to an order on soft slider buns.

Steakburger sandwiches

Fresh-pressed steakburger patty, griddled to order, dressed simple so the beef does the work.

Loaded fries

Six different builds on top of the fries everyone keeps writing reviews about. Rotates with what we're feeling that week.

Gourmet salads

Hand-chopped greens, real toppings, dressings made in-house. The lighter side of the menu and a regular sellout.
