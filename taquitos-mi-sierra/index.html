The list

What we make.

Mini Tacos

Three meats to choose from: beef, chicken, or pastor. Served on doubled corn tortillas with onion, cilantro, and house salsa.

Quesabirria

Birria-dipped tortillas crisped with melted cheese and tender beef, plated with a cup of consomé on the side.

Large Quesadillas

Hand-stretched flour tortillas folded over your choice of meat with melted cheese. Add avocado for two dollars.

Flour Tacos

Warm flour tortillas built to order. Same meats as the mini tacos, sized up for a full plate.

Tortas

Toasted bolillo loaded with meat, beans, lettuce, tomato, and avocado. Add cheese for two dollars.

Event Catering

Corporate lunches, schools, weddings, birthdays. Tell us headcount and menu, we bring the truck and the crew.
