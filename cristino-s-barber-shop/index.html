How the chair runs in Parma

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Skin fade and beard line-up
Tight zero fade up the sides, scissor work on top, beard squared with the razor. About forty minutes in the chair.
Classic taper and hot towel
Conservative taper, scissor finish on top, hot towel and aftershave around the ears and neck. The everyday cut.
Straight razor shave
Full face shave, two passes with the straight razor, hot lather, cold towel finish. Reserve the chair in advance.
Kid's cut, under 12
Patient cut for first-timers and squirmers. Clippers or scissors, parent picks the length, no rush from the chair.
Gray blending
Color matched to existing gray to soften the line, not cover it. Sits about three weeks before it grows out.
