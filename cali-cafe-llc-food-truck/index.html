The list

What we make.

Cali chicken bowl

Grilled chicken, cilantro lime rice, black beans, pico, and avocado crema, the truck's best seller since day one.

Baja fish tacos

Two corn tortillas, beer-battered or grilled fish, cabbage slaw, and chipotle crema, served with a lime wedge.

Turkey avocado club

Roasted turkey, bacon, avocado, and pepper jack on toasted sourdough, wrapped to travel if you're eating at your desk.

Iced horchata latte

House-made horchata blended with espresso over ice, one of the few things on the menu you won't find anywhere else in Monroe.

Loaded breakfast burrito

Scrambled eggs, potatoes, cheese, and your choice of chorizo or bacon, wrapped tight, served until we run out.
