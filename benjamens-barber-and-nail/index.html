About

How we run the chair in Saint Pete Beach

We opened Benjamens because most shops on the beach either run on a long wait list or push you out the door in fifteen minutes. We wanted a chair you could walk into, get a real cut, and have your partner get her nails done at the same time. Saint Pete Beach is a small town and we treat it like one.

Meet Owner

The owner has been cutting hair on the Gulf side of Pinellas for years and opened Benjamens to put a real barber chair and a nail station under the same roof. Walk-ins welcome, appointments respected, no upsells at the chair.
