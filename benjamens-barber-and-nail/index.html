How the shop runs in Saint Pete Beach

Walk-ins welcome · Calls preferred

Skin fade + beard line-up
Bald fade through the sides, scissor work on top, beard trimmed and edged with a straight razor finish. Roughly forty-five minutes.
Father and son cut
Two haircuts back to back in the same chair. Kid goes first while dad waits, both walk out clean for a flat combo rate.
Gel manicure
Full prep, gel base, two coats of color, gel top, cured under the lamp. Holds up through beach days and dish nights for two to three weeks.
Spa pedicure
Longer soak, sugar scrub, paraffin or mask, hot towel wrap, polish or gel. Built for people who stand on their feet all day.
Wedding morning package
Groom haircut and beard, plus manicure for the bride or mother of the bride. Booked as a single morning slot, coffee included.

A note from Owner

The shop is run by a husband and wife team, barber on one side, nail tech on the other, both behind the chair full time. They picked the corner on Gulf Boulevard because every other shop on the beach was booked two weeks out, and they wanted somewhere a walk-in could still get a clean cut before dinner.

— Owner
