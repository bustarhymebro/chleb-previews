The list

What we make.

Birria tacos + consomme

Three slow-braised beef tacos on dipped tortillas, fried crisp, served with a cup of rich broth for dipping.

Street elotes

Grilled corn on the cob rolled in crema, cotija, chili powder, and a squeeze of lime.

Loaded nachos

Tortilla chips piled with beans, seasoned beef or chicken, jalapeños, pico de gallo, and crema.

Quesabirria plate

Two quesabirria folded with Oaxacan cheese and braised beef, pressed on the griddle, with side consomme.

Party botanas platter

Chips, three salsas, guacamole, and elote dip, sized for six to ten people, available with 24-hour notice.

A short note

From Lupe.

Lupe started El Botanas after years of feeding her neighborhood out of her kitchen and realizing the truck could reach more people than the kitchen ever would. She makes the salsas fresh every morning before the truck opens, and the birria braise runs overnight. If something is sold out, she will tell you before you finish ordering.

— Lupe

Book El Botanas Food Truck.

We started El Botanas because Huntington deserved a Mexican street food option that did not come out of a steam tray or a bag. Most of the lunch options around town run on a fixed menu and a long line, and by noon the good stuff is already gone. We build everything to order, park where the people are, and keep the menu tight enough that every item on the board is worth ordering. Lupe takes the catering calls herself.
