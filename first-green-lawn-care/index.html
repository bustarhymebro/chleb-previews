The plan list

How we run yards in Charlotte

  • Weekly mow + edge
    Cut, trim, edge, blow. Standard 7-day rotation March through October, 14-day in shoulder months. Flat monthly rate.
  • Spring cleanup package
    Bed cleanout, light prune, pre-emergent on turf, first mulch refresh. One visit, scheduled late February through April.
  • Fall leaf removal
    Full property leaf clearing, bed and gutter blowout, hauled off site. Booked as a one-off or a three-visit late-season block.
  • Mulch refresh
    Bed re-edge, weed pull, two to three inches of hardwood or pine straw. Quoted by the yard with delivery included.
  • Shrub trim + haul
    Hand prune front and side beds, shape hedges, bag and remove all clippings. Quarterly or one-time before an event.
