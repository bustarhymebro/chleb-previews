The list

What we make.

Shrimp pad thai

Rice noodles, tamarind, peanuts, lime, scallion, bean sprouts. Shrimp tossed in last so they stay tender.

Basil chicken

Ground chicken stir-fried with Thai basil, chili, and garlic. Served over jasmine rice with fried rice on the side.

Red curry

Coconut milk, red curry paste, bamboo shoots, bell pepper. Pick your protein. Heat dialed to your liking.

Thai chicken

House marinade, seared on the flattop, finished with sweet chili sauce on the side. Comes with rice.

Spring rolls

Hand-rolled, fried to order, sweet chili for dipping. Order them while you wait on the main plate.
